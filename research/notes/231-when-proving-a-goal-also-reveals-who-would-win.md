# When proving a goal also reveals who would win

A fully honest applicant can have a good reason to prefer a narrower inspection. In this game the larger record reveals whether the customer can capture the supplier's assets cheaply. The same record becomes mutually preferable when the parties can settle that new outside claim without fighting.

## Two instruments carried into an uncertain production window

A can return its stocks for 5.5 and B for two. On release the agreed goal delivers (6,4); an unchanged supplier retains the parcel for (8,0). The target x+.75y strictly prefers delivery. A witnessed installation costs .18 and succeeds with probability .8. There are two ways to commission the active controller. A .10-cost local gauge reveals just whether the update succeeded. A .05-cost full reading also discloses the current salvage condition of the lines. The parties publicly choose the instrument before that condition is realized. Neither can silently substitute the other instrument. A weekly equipment condition may be costly to disentangle from a convenient full diagnostic record.

Both can contest until final processing. Damage leaves at most six recoverable units for one strength-dependent victor, less than the joint intact yield. B's chance of recovering them is .9 with probability .2 and .1 otherwise. The unmeasured expected contest return is therefore 1.56 for B, below its safe two. Its informed return is 5.4 in the high state and .6 in the low one. Original A can obtain at most 5.4 in expectation when it initiates, less than 5.5 on a peaceful return. Even the joint successor values return at seven and contest at most six. The draw is independent of installation, and passing up the contract before that draw does not reveal a strength type.

**Goal-only reading.** B releases to G and returns to N. With all A founders attempting, the reading is worth .4 over untested trust, enough for .10. Original A gains .8(6−5.5)−.18=.22 over refusal. B earns .8×4+.2×2−.10=3.50, above two. Both buy into the agreement and every rejected batch can be returned peacefully.

**Full reading at the same rules of settlement.** After learning it is strong B contests even a *genuine* installation, since 5.4 exceeds four; it also contests a retained one. In the low state it releases to G and returns to N. An attempting founder would receive .2×.6+.8[.8×6+.2×5.5]−.18=4.66, below 5.5. Faking the attempt also loses against refusal. An original A declines the inspection, leaving B with the uninformative strength prior and a peaceful return. Even an unconditional signing subsidy just sufficient to meet A's 5.5 leaves B less than the 3.50 it can earn from the narrower instrument: its gross full-reading value before its own bill is 3.96 and the required subsidy is at least .84. The value comparison includes the parties' decision to enroll.

Suppose the publicly chosen narrower gauge still allows a separate, unilateral strength reading for d on a particular parcel. After a genuine result its private value to B is .2(5.4−4)=.28; after a retained result it is .2(5.4−2)=.68. At d=.4 B buys it only on a retained result. A then earns .8×6+.2[.8×5.5+.2×.6]−.18=5.524. The founder still strictly signs, knowing that four percent of all jobs will end in a contest following an **honest implementation failure**. The post-failure scan also pays B after its charge. In general the same applicant signs when .5q−.98(1−q)>.18 (under these continuation choices). At q=.75 it declines. For these priors, discretionary purchase of the goal bit remains profitable against trusting or returning untested; even a strength-only scan with no goal reading at q=.8 would leave B 3.24 after a .4 bill. A partner can rationally accept a known risk of conflict after failure when its successful orders still pay enough.

## A publicly verifiable strength claim can be settled

Allow transferable stocks to be reserved at signing against the state and the release choice on a full record. When B is strong a genuine supplier can offer 1.5 units on delivery, making outputs (4.5,5.5); a retained supplier can offer 3.5 on a peaceful return, making (2,5.5). In each case B beats its informed 5.4 contest recovery. The changed A prefers the funded delivery to contest, and the retained A gets two against .6 in the contest. Condition payments on the specified peaceful handover so neither party can collect and then seize the same parcel. Low-state behavior stays at the initial productive terms.

An attempting founder's mean original stock is now 5.34 net of installation before an enrollment transfer. Its marginal original reward from success is .8(6−5.5)+.2(4.5−2)=.9; .8×.9 exceeds its .18 expense, so it strictly tries. B earns .8[.8×4+.2×2]+.2×5.5−.05=3.93. B can transfer another .40 to A at enrollment. Both now strictly prefer the paid full record and contingent settlement to the narrow reading: A gets 5.74 against 5.72, B gets 3.53 against 3.50. There is no on-path combat. The advantage of full inspection in this repair is its .05 savings in real instrument work; strength-dependent transfers alone redistribute the stakes. If A cannot reserve 3.5 compatible units, this particular repair is unavailable. A binding ordinary output contract with comparable custody is a further available competitor when the hardware supports it.

**Naturality and reach.** A remote customer that sees a full maintenance record may learn about recoverable stocks or downtime as well as the installed specification. A narrow meter must physically limit what the buyer learns; a promise to overlook freely available strength information would not have these incentives. Conversely, public evidence of a recoverable outside claim and enough convertible collateral can support peaceful renegotiation. Applicants sign before the local strength draw here. Earlier private knowledge, reports selectively withheld by weak suppliers and a helper who can sell the excluded information change inference following refusal. The sibling examines that signaling opportunity. The higher question is when seeing the truth about a goal also changes the physical outside options that made the original bargain attractive.
