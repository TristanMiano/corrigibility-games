# How much is a reliable boundary around a negotiated goal worth?

**E8, note 180.** If one kind of job makes the whole reciprocal partnership profitable and a superficially identical request comes from outside that partnership, the founders can price information about its source. Here there is an exact value over all policies that use the available evidence. The founders can realize it by choosing what they want done on positive and negative readings. The same value prices a matched conventional controller.

## Two reasons to see the same local transaction

As in 179, the original parties use the lower expectation of their own production under the two workload laws. With probability 1-s the job is a regular X or Y opportunity. Conditional on that event set p=12/19. In X the low A-to-B service costs one and gives 3/4 with probability p; the reverse specialist service costs one and gives two otherwise. Reverse roles in Y. Full regular service yields 2/19 to each founder under either law. This choice of p equalizes the two original returns, allowing a sharp value formula. Nearby p preserve strict decisions with slack as in 179.

With probability s a new request instead arises outside the regular program, equally likely in either direction. It offers the same physical low-yield transfer as a regular low job. Each accepted outside request loses one quarter of aggregate useful output. Once a job is offered its actual direction and physical payoffs are known. A cheap channel that presents only those facts cannot distinguish a regular low job from the new request. This is an incentive-relevant version of shared equipment serving different programs. The duty to handle a request can be physically the same while the rest of its productive portfolio differs. A reputable registry may attest its source. Treating every outside call as intrinsically harmful would be unwarranted in a different portfolio.

Let the publicly obtainable evidence have law P(z) on regular jobs, the same for either direction and productivity tier, and Q(z) on outside jobs, independent of direction there. Finite signals suffice. These probabilities describe operational records, with possible false inclusion and mistaken exclusion. A genuine authentication service, fixed protected reference outputs and binding preauthorization to read the evidence have real startup and maintenance costs. If the would-be applicant can select or substitute records, use the induced strategic law as in 176.

## An exact price over an entire information class

The largest original value guaranteed to both founders by any policy using those observations is

    Σ_z [(1-s)(2/19) P(z) - (s/8) Q(z)]_+.                           (1)

For the upper bound grant a planner arbitrary authority to implement the desired transfers. Average its policy with the policy obtained by interchanging both people and workload laws. A guarantee that each founder receives a given amount in each law is preserved by this publicly randomized symmetry. Conditional on z the resulting low-job acceptance probability a_z is the same in either direction. It applies also to the outside low jobs, since these cannot be distinguished locally from their regular counterparts. Write x_z for high acceptance. On regular X the two conditional gains are -pa_z+2(1-p)x_z and (3/4)pa_z-(1-p)x_z. Take weights 1/3 and 2/3 as in 179. The high probability cancels. Conditional on a nuisance job each founder instead gets -a_z/8. Combining these bounds and maximizing 0≤a_z≤1 gives (1). The same upper bound applies to lotteries over arbitrarily nonlinear goals, separate controllers, signatures and mere promises if their power to observe and change outcomes lies within this class. An additional informative signal, transferable payment or improved physical recipe must be priced separately.

For attainment accept **all** jobs with a positive bracket in (1), and none with a negative one. With p=12/19, regular gains on an accepted reading are exactly 2/19 for both founders in both laws. The symmetric nuisance loss is exactly 1/8. This also explains what an actual controlling goal can do: on approved readings use max(q_A,q_B)+η(q_A+q_B); on other readings use min(q_A,q_B)+η(q_A+q_B), with 0<η<1 and equal initial protected stocks. Under the first criterion the increase in the maximum outweighs the loss on a low job. Under the second the decrease in the minimum outweighs even the aggregate gain of a high job. Every prescribed nontrivial job decision is strict, whether initiated by the donor or approved by the recipient. Neither site has to retain a policy solely through indifference to later offers. Monotonicity in each output is preserved. The criteria can be defined over project-specific changes from known baselines, at the real price of certifying them. A common positive cost or actual physical loss on an extraneous contest makes productive action or keeping preferable on the indicated histories, assuming it does not shift valuable outside rights.

Without information (1) is max{0,(1-s)2/19-s/8}, so s≥16/35 makes even the most flexible untagged policy unable to give both founders any strict guarantee. With perfect tags each can obtain (1-s)2/19 before setup. In between, a reading is worth admitting exactly when its likelihood ratio of outside to regular work is below (16/19)(1-s)/s, with regular-impossible readings closed. The pre-formation value of finer evidence is the sum of the increases in positive parts; a refinement helps precisely when it separates positive and negative brackets inside some former observation.

At s=1/2 take a paid positive readback with P(+)=0.90 and Q(+)=0.10. The negative bracket is strictly negative. The honest original return from admitting positives is 0.9/19-0.1/16≈0.04112 per founder. Suppose each can configure the finite conditional criterion for 0.012, an alternative outside booking pays each 0.005, and A can procure the common readback for 0.020 on completion of both signatures. Then A expects about 0.00912 net and B about 0.02912. Both choose the funded offer over the outside booking and over the entire untagged class. If a binding ordinary local controller is available at 0.008 per site, it can buy the same readback and beat the genuine arrangement for both. A change in the price of an actual observation or evaluator can therefore determine both the offer made and whether it contains genuine revisions. Devices that never see a decision-relevant reading must use their actual information. No one first has to value authenticity, cooperation or the other site's independent output.

**Exploration ledger.** The framing candidate concerns the negotiable boundary of what a future self values. The structural candidate prices evidence by the original gain on an actual regular portfolio less the loss on similar outside requests. The untagged nonlinear repair in 179 remains successful there; extending it unchanged into the new instance with s=1/2 loses the original guarantee. A sibling buys a source-sensitive criterion or a conventional authorized gate. A higher-rung successor asks how much participants will spend to preserve the provenance of growing classes of future projects after prospective applicants can influence admission tests.
