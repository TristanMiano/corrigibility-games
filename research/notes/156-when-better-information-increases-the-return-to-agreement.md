# Which extra information would the founders pay to acquire?

**E8, note 156.** A cooperative agreement can be easier to justify when its future operators can tell which jobs actually merit a referral. A forecast is valuable for that purpose when it changes a decision on a route that matters to the least well-served founders. Simply predicting a quantity more accurately may leave every relevant choice unchanged.

## Information available at production

Take the directed opportunity class of 153. In a realized state let Δ be the vector of original-utility differences between referring and keeping, with -d for the donor and r for the recipient. Values are bounded, d,r>0 and other entries zero. The current assignment controller observes a signal Z including the route identity; it can use any conditional probability between zero and one for referral, before observing any unrevealed yields. The state distribution and signal technology are known. Costed future decisions, learning and resource loss are folded into these increments where the representation permits them. A common objective can use these observations, as can a binding ordinary delegate with the same access. Private unverified reports are a different interface, as shown by 143–148.

For a vector p of nonnegative weights with Σp_i=1 and original reservations k, define

    H_Z(p) = E[(E[p·Δ | Z])_+] - p·k.

The largest common surplus above those reservations is σ_Z=min_p H_Z(p). This follows as in (153.2): at each observation the maximizer of the weighted sum chooses the locally superior expected option. For an arbitrary bounded type law, the set of [0,1]-valued measurable policies is convex and weakly compact in L², being closed, convex and bounded in this Hilbert space. Its image under the finitely many continuous expected-payoff linear functionals is compact and convex. Minimax or finite-dimensional separation of that image gives the same formula. The positive-supporting-weight construction from 153 therefore also furnishes a common objective implementing a strictly improving policy whenever σ_Z>0. It responds to expected merits at the site's actual information, with the same observation cost as its comparator.

Suppose Z' refines Z and is available **after signing, before production**. The old policy can be simulated and conditional Jensen for the positive part gives H_Z'(p)≥H_Z(p) at every p. The inequality σ_Z'>σ_Z holds exactly when every minimizing p of the old expression admits a positive-probability old signal cell in the following sense: conditional on Z, the new posterior score E[p·Δ|Z'] has both a strictly positive and a strictly negative probability on a positive-measure set of Z realizations. Indeed positive-part Jensen is strict precisely on sign crossing. Both objectives are continuous in p on a compact simplex. Equality of minima occurs iff some old minimizer also sees zero gain from the extra information. This is a criterion at the actual bottleneck, not a count of more accurate predictions.

If a particular founder b can pay a fee F in its own original output for the common instrument, compare with the actual attainable old payoffs o_i. Set the other setup reservations accordingly and call the resulting function H'_o. An improving agreement that also funds b exists iff the minimum of H'_o(p)-p_b F is positive. In particular faces with p_b=0 must have positive scores. Subject to that, the limiting sponsor price is the infimum of H'_o(p)/p_b over p_b>0. A sponsor still needs control of procurement, a binding prior subscription or a current-goal incentive to execute it, and willing counterparties. Gains that can be shifted to the sponsor through an actually available route can change this price.

## Which kinds of physical variation suffice?

For each edge with variable positive d,r and bounded r/d, let g_e^max be the essential supremum of r/d conditional on that edge under full information. At zero fixed expense the source-cycle test of 153 applies using g_e^max for strict expansion. If a source lacks such a cycle, its positive potential weights satisfy p_j r≤p_i d almost surely on all internal events; summing weighted gains gives the same obstruction. If every source has an expanding cycle of essential suprema, choose slightly smaller positive ratios on its edges that still yield a product exceeding one. There is positive-probability opportunity on each edge meeting its chosen ratio and positive expected donor capacity there. Restrict referral to those events and run the scaled cycle-and-path construction; the actual incoming yields weakly exceed the chosen lower bounds. The remaining tree edges only need positive realizable gains. Thus sufficiently small but strictly positive gains are possible. The construction can rely on rare favorable states. The quantity H_Z and its explicit implementation expenses distinguish a useful recurring opportunity from an existence result with vanishing returns.

For a homogeneous n-cycle with equal chance 1/n per edge and a rotationally symmetric law of (d,r), the minimizer of H_Z with equal reservations can be taken uniform: average all cyclic shifts of any minimizer and use convexity. With edge identity but no information about relative quality, each founder's optimum gross common margin is (E[r-d])_+/n. With public quality measurements it is E[(r-d)_+]/n. More generally substitute the conditional mean given the measurement. For example local service yields one and remote service yields either 1/4 or 7/4, equally likely. Without quality information there is no strict common gain; with accurate post-agreement measurement each gains 3/(8n). With a symmetric binary sensor correct with probability a>1/2, the posterior positive score on a favorable reading is (3/4)(2a-1); the pre-fee common margin is 3(2a-1)/(8n). An equally priced sensor that predicts only the *absolute* level of two proportional returns while preserving the sign of r-d has zero such decision premium.

Absolute utility scales can hide what physical variation is needed. If donor and recipient values factor as local scale factors times an invariant job yield, e.g. g_ij=(v_j/v_i)(1-loss_ij), every cycle product is at most one at positive routing loss. Client-specific timing, actual production complements, or a change in marginal utility across states can break that factorization. Agents can search for such a partner, pay to measure the state, or build a compatible connector as in 154. Whether they would do so depends on the funded threshold, not just an edge's theoretical maximum.

**Empirical limits.** BorrowDirect began as a three-institution partnership and later expanded. MIT reported 5,052 books lent and 3,352 borrowed across its resource-sharing services in fiscal 2023. Those observations support real cross-institutional referrals and unequal volumes. They cannot identify which three institutions form a particular return cycle, the private cost and value of each transaction, or whether these institutions would want changed goals. National Library of Australia's cost recovery and digital substitution also give ordinary ways to handle a persistent net lender and avoid some physical movement. These examples involve renewable exchanges and public missions, which are important alternative explanations of actual participation. **Exploration ledger.** Framing: increasing capability can make instrumental accommodation easier to discover and use. Structural: study how the actual observations change the attainable set of all founders' current-goal payoffs. The sign-crossing test is promising for information bought for use after formation. The claim that a more detailed forecast always increases the adoption budget is dead at the constant-sign instance. A sibling builds a sensor that can reverse the relevant decision. A structural successor distinguishes common production information from privately learned pre-contract information about who would bear the cost. The latter can move the date or even the feasibility of genuine adoption.
