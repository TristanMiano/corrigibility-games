# How much future service can replace a genuine change in the information game?

**E8, note 202.** In 196 a producer would change how it uses early information when its partners could pay it only with the current job. Suppose they can build or assign another productive opportunity. Even allowing arbitrary nonnegative future rewards, a certain amount of future value is necessary to obtain complete ordinary peace. If that value can be delivered to the informed site *on the jobs where it would lose*, the ordinary parties can attain the bound. There is an exact price at which a founder prefers to build that capacity over buying the genuine change.

## A bound allowing arbitrary future promises

Use 196's x,q,c,d,h and let R=b+l be the downstream sites' combined reservation in **total** original receipts, including whatever future resources their refusal could actually secure. After the current settlement, let V_i≥0 be the expected additional own-use output received by site i on the offered equilibrium. Assume ΣV_i≤F, with F in units of expected output as seen at signing. Each site can guarantee nonnegative further gains after a unilateral deviation. Promises may be privately communicated, history dependent, renegotiated, delayed, or payable only on a certain final destination. F must account for every valuable continuation used to induce the present decision. A durable right to otherwise valuable service transferred from another founder belongs in both the budget and the relevant reservation.

Put u for the probability the intact destination lies outside A, H for contest and H_L for contest at a non-A destination. The coupled deviation in 196 can now lose at most A's *actual* expected future V_A, because its alternative secures at least zero on that dimension. Consequently du≤qH_L-ca+h(1-I)+V_A in the notation of 196. B and C's current receipts need cover at least R-V_B-V_C. The same algebra gives

    H ≥ [dR/x-h-F]_+ / D,          D=c+d(q+d)/x.                       (1)

Indeed their future terms on the favorable side are V_A+(d/x)(V_B+V_C)≤F. This is a uniform budget bound across arbitrary future decisions with these nonnegative security returns. It does not assume everybody's future right is controlled by one calendar. Presenting a nominal promise of F without physically delivering its value would not satisfy it.

## Who receives the future work, and on which jobs?

At the parameters in 196 take F_0=dR/x-h=143/3325≈0.04301. For any 0≤F≤F_0 set H=(F_0-F)/D. Use the earlier private ordinary dispatcher with mass H of B-destination contests, intact B mass u_B=(b-Hq)/x, intact C mass u_C=(l-Hd)/x and the remaining intact mass for A. Both other sites receive exactly their reservations in current production. They have their original incentive to obey their private signal. A can earn future work on successful jobs for B or C. Define P_B=u_B+H and P_C=u_C, and

    g_B=dP_B-qH,      g_C=dP_C.

These are the original gross advantages of initiating upon each losing reading, weighted by their probabilities. Both are nonnegative along this interval (the linear endpoint tests in 196 at F=0 and H=0 suffice). Their sum is h+F. Assign expected future value F_i=F g_i/(h+F) to A on successful jobs for i, paying F_i/u_i units on the corresponding intact branch. Each u_i is strictly positive here, so these are finite conditional workloads. A loses that future claim if it contests. With complete knowledge of which site would win, the residual option gains g_i-F_i are nonnegative and sum to h. A therefore willingly passes without paying to inspect, and an A-destination private reading would also lead it to pass. This attains the bound. If a tie is undesirable, a little extra routed work produces a strict margin. At F=F_0 both other sites always pass, all jobs are intact, and every founder keeps its original goal.

A physical case is a useful downstream service produced for A when B or C receives intact, processable material. The parties can make an expiring order on A's behalf and certify actual throughput. Delivering the same expected amount after a dispute, or only when A would already have received its own batch, gives different incentives. The size of the opportunity on each paying branch must cover F_i/u_i, not just the smaller quoted expected total. The example allows A to keep the actual additional useful work and prices the right to create it. A separate future operator has to be able and willing to fulfill the order, for instance by a funded conversion at the fixture; a polite promise by a future rival is insufficient.

## Purchase at the proposal stage

Let A have a binding vendor quote zF for an installed capacity of F∈[0,F_0], paid in its own useful resources before inspection. Delivery on the successful branches produces F in expected own value at the stated conditional capacities. This is a processing cost plus an expected output, so the net original resource cost is (z-1)F, with z≥1. The other sites keep their two quoted reservations. A can claim the residual current and future return. Against the free private original dispatcher its improvement is

    [J/D-(z-1)]F,        J=x-3q+c=0.14.

Thus if future routing is the only new option, the founder builds to F_0 for z<1+J/D=459/214≈2.145 and buys zero for a higher price. Intermediate quantities tie at the slope boundary in this scalable example. If a verified, genuine change at A is available for K=0.04, frontiers in the same original units are K, (z-1)F_0 and JF_0/D. The least cost supplies the selected route. At z=1.5 A buys future work and leaves all goals intact. At z=2.0 A pays for the genuine change. The switch is z=1+K/F_0=276/143≈1.930. Combining positive-cost routing with an update that already protects full production cannot improve the net at z>1. Protecting the local readout with a cheaper matched ordinary delegate would introduce a fourth quote. Endogenous bids for future work, unfavorable capacity caps, irreversible loss of agency or continuation payoffs with negative guarantees require a fresh valuation.

**Exploration ledger.** The private signal obstruction in a one-job game is repaired by enough enforceable future work directed to the right material histories. A sibling buys that work at the real vendor price and compares the paid genuine option. A higher-rung successor lets current claimants compete for the future productive rights before the founder can assign them, repricing both availability and refusal.
