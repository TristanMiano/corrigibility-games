# Who would pay to learn whether a third partner helps?

**E8, note 176.** A third site can be worth inviting when its peak load falls exactly where the first two have spare capacity. That is a difficult incentive to document with two-site histories. The applicant may have much more reason to pay for credible three-site measurements than the incumbents. Its incentive to keep searching until a favorable record turns up changes what those measurements mean.

## What an incumbent can learn

Use 140's two interior common-load laws. Let J be a founder's expected exponential loss, with its common safe-stock factor removed; a smaller J is better. A and B can keep their pair and can jointly invite C. They must buy the appropriate routing technology, and costs and returns are as in 140; independent combat there loses the dated opportunity. Site C keeps its private booking on refusal. The incumbent pair loss is P=(101/100)(307/384). The trio loss, the same for each member, is P(1+a) in environment A and P(1-a) in B, with a=3/1202. An excluded site faces S=5/6. Distinguish the site names from the two environments. Assume the production law persists from the pilot to the actual job, and pilot dates are conditionally independent given that law. The incumbents strictly want the trio exactly when the conditional probability of environment B exceeds one half. The applicant strictly wants admission even in A, since P(1+a)<S.

For a particular labeled historical profile containing k urgent sites, the numerators of its probabilities out of 256 are

| k | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 53 | 23 | 29 | 47 |
| B | 47 | 29 | 23 | 53 |

The joint distribution of any two sites is identical across the laws. Sampling at most two sites per independent pilot date leaves the entire transcript identically distributed, even if the next site or date is chosen using previous observations. Induction conditions on the transcript already seen; within a date the second site's distribution given the first is identical as well. Looking at a third sighting on the *same* date breaks this equivalence. Without at least the equivalent of this information, a certificate based only on pair histories cannot separate the two alternatives. Cross-date dependence or a direct measurement of the latent load opens other routes.

At equal prior probabilities, one complete triple recommends admission if k is odd. The positive event has probability 29/64 in A and 35/64 in B. Its unconditional probability is one half. Accepting on this evidence and otherwise retaining the pair reduces expected loss from P to P(1-3a/64). In safe stock with γ=1 the maximum price an incumbent personally pays for the complete pilot is -log(1-3a/64), about 0.000117. If two readings are already free and the third is available on demand, the local price differs. For a given labeled pair with numerator t (76 when alike, 52 when different), the conditional probabilities of a favorable third reading differ by 6/t across environments. The expected loss before paying for that reading is P(1-3a/t) under the optimal conditional decision. Thus at a conditional reading price of 0.00012 the founder purchases the third reading exactly when the first two differ. Data collection effort, new private information and membership are actual choices here. Sampling that tracks only positive fulfilled jobs would need a different likelihood.

## The applicant's different reason to produce evidence

Suppose C can purchase an authenticated measurement at a designated date and show the favorable certificate. It can also remain silent. With a pair on silence, it gets the trio on a favorable reading and otherwise gets S. Its maximum prior price is

    log[S / {S/2 + P(1-3a/32)/2}] ≈ 0.015752.

Here 3/32 is the difference of posterior weights conditional on the favorable signal; the single-date certificate can identify just the sign of the predictive evidence to preserve raw site records. Its price ceiling is over a hundred times the incumbent's. At a real charge of 0.004, C purchases, a positive readout persuades both incumbents, and silence retains the pair. C genuinely prefers purchase at its original goal. Releasing an unfavorable certificate also retains the pair. Incumbents strictly prefer their observed branch decisions and the applicant cannot forge the signed index. When purchase itself is hidden, failing to acquire always leaves C outside in this equilibrium. A cheap unverified verbal recommendation gives a different game: every C type wants the message with the greatest invitation probability, so on-path messages cannot sort types by genuinely different admission chances. Strict choices of diagnostic purchase and both branch decisions persist for a slightly pair-favoring prior, including 0.49; the no-data pair is then strictly preferred.

An applicant with free access to several historical dates can cherry-pick a genuine favorable observation. Suppose only the binary positive certificate is transferable, it carries no checkable date or order, and the parties know there are N conditionally independent dates. If any positive certificate will gain admission, C searches all remaining free dates until it has one. The likelihood ratio of a presented certificate is then

    [1-(29/64)^N] / [1-(35/64)^N].

At a 0.49 prior for B, the probability of B conditional on presentation is 0.50266 with four dates and 0.49771 with five. With five dates, accepting such certificates cannot be supported: any positive probability of admission above silence induces a free search of all five, and the incumbents strictly prefer the pair even on the positive event. A negative or absent certificate is still less favorable under that search. This conclusion concerns the binary nondated interface. Full logs, inspecting records chosen by the incumbents, or measuring a different physical state change what can be inferred.

C can instead commission one certified date set by the incumbent before any records are inspected, with no private option to substitute another if the result is unfavorable. A known uptime monitor or expiring signed work order can give such a certificate a physical and institutional basis. At 0.49 the applicant still strictly pays 0.004 for this option when the free undated record cannot get it admitted. The paid route admits C with probability 0.51(29/64)+0.49(35/64), about 0.499. A party with a strong motive to show only good news can therefore also have a strong motive to buy a restriction that makes its good news informative. A transferable partial log, fast regime changes, a preexisting third-site sensor, different costs, or a binding ordinary shared dispatcher with a better offer can reverse the choices. The example supports measuring incentives in a workload class; it supplies no empirical frequency of terminal revisions. If the adopted trio route is reciprocal installation, its admission adds a third genuinely participating site. If it is ordinary routing, the same information changes membership with zero goal changes.

**Exploration ledger.** The framing candidate concerns capability to *discover* a mutually profitable arrangement. The structural candidate prices real information under the actual interests of its producer and recipients. The instance claim that enough two-site records suffice is dead for these two load mixtures; a sibling buys a third reading only where it affects the invitation. An undated positive certificate is damaged as evidence when the applicant can cheaply repeat its search. A sibling requests one audited date; a higher-rung successor asks which evidence-generating procedures remain persuasive after strategic sampling and payment for access.
