# 274 — Who would pay to reassign the available installation equipment?

The same strengths and skills can support very different distributions of trust and fighting. That observation alone does not give anyone the right to change the pairing. Here three existing pairs retain their physical sites, vetoes and outside opportunities. A contractor can move their three installation kits before the founders decide what to do. It must secure the consent of *every original founder*, including a powerful customer losing access to its best kit.

Use the three equal-mass sites of [273](273-what-skill-and-strength-surveys-can-say-about-a-population.md), once each. The weak, balanced and strong customers currently have kits with q=(.24,.27,.30), respectively. Each site already has rights to those terms and uses the specified active assessment when supported. Each supplier can know its own attempt cost k before consenting. Neither the contractor nor the buyers can verifiably condition relocation payments on k. A uniform signing price per named party is available from separately held, fungible stocks. After the swap the local plants still have each of the original choices, renewed conflict remains physically available, and an early signing payment is sunk. Kit portability while preserving each assigned q, common processing deadlines, credible public assays and full buy-in from incumbent titleholders are the main institutional assumptions.

For a test-using cell, write A_N,B_N for refusal returns under a retained supplier and A_G,B_G under a genuine one. Let π=q²Z/K on these uncapped branches. The supplier's original payoff at k and the buyer's original payoff are, respectively,

    A_N+.1(6−A_N)+max(qZ−k,0);
    B_N+π(B_G−B_N)+.9πg−.1(1−π)h−.10.

With no test or trade the two receive their retained-state fallbacks. For each old→new site pair compute the infimum ΔA over k and the buyer difference ΔB. If supplier payoffs are base_i+max(cutoff_i−k,0), the infimum difference is new base minus old base plus min(0,new cutoff−old cutoff), since k covers both cutoffs. The largest total relocation bill these named incumbents can strictly afford with type-independent, balanced side payments is the sum of the minima and buyer differences, approached from below. Necessity follows by summing each party's own veto. Sufficiency follows by transferring gains from parties above their required minimum to those below and dividing any strictly positive surplus. Sufficient separate stocks are needed to carry out the transfers.

Suppose every new deterministic routing requires one common calibration and transport run costing .42, while keeping existing assignments is free. **Only (.27,.30,.24) can buy guaranteed consent** among these assignments. Relative to existing rights the weak site's original suppliers gain at least .315 and its buyer gains .06570. The balanced site gains at least .400 and .042 respectively. At the strong site the largest supplier loss is .15120 and the buyer loses .20295. One feasible settlement collects .310 and .060 at the weak site, and .390 and .035 at the balanced one, from supplier and buyer respectively. It pays the strong supplier .156, the strong buyer .207 and the contractor .432. Total receipts equal disbursements, the contractor earns .012 after real cost and all six founders strictly consent even at their least favorable k. Private project goods alone could not make these transfers if no one could exchange them.

On the resulting local assessments all three sites have some verified trade. Across the three offers genuine adoption rises from .05400 to .07558 per applicant, while combat falls from .59013 to .56243 per offer. Confidence among released partners falls from .63502 to .42391 because more uncertain offers are worth making. These figures describe an institution participants actually have reason to construct under these prices. Mere possession of portable kits or the existence of an attractive assignment would establish less. If a market coordinates on refusal after the move, the receipts must be recomputed; [268](268-how-a-buyer-can-start-trade-from-distrust.md) gives one priced way to select positive trade in a related local game. This broker inherits the stated local operating convention.

## Who prefers a different assignment?

A neighboring transit operator values its own undamaged network. Each local dispute imposes an additional expected loss L on its independently exposed stock. It has no role in a site's final handover, but it can publicly promise a fixed subsidy *before* the broker assigns the kits. For a broker selecting the highest attainable guaranteed surplus subject to the old rights, the gap between the all-active route and (.27,.24,.30) is .0878464 in total across the three jobs. The latter route avoids .046656 expected disputes across the same three jobs, the smallest aggregate combat rate among the six. The operator can strictly buy the lower-combat route when L>.0878464/.046656≈1.883. At L=3 a .10 conditional subsidy pays the broker more on that route and saves the operator about .140 in expected disruption. Fees can leave strict, arbitrarily small concessions below each consenting type's maximum. The subsidized route has genuine adoption .06502, above the original .054 but below .07558 under the all-active route. Combat falls to .54688 per offer.

This purchase presumes the operator bargains before anyone acquires rights to the new allocation. Buying it *after* the balanced plant owns the .30 kit would require new payments to that plant. A public fee triggered by an actual future dispute could instead change the local combat incentives. The fixed reallocation subsidy leaves those incentives intact. Participation under the existing private cost types, public test and transfer titles, and the cost of a van that must service a common calibration route are all concrete restrictions. Cheaper pairwise transport, tradable future output, a credible ordinary router or voluntary offers contingent on private skill would change the ranking. These conditions have plausible analogues in a shared equipment pool, while their numerical frequencies remain an open empirical question.
