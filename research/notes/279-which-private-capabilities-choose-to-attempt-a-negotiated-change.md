# 279 — Which private capabilities choose to attempt a negotiated change?

There is a common restriction on what the many receipt and warranty bargains can do. If a factory privately knows its installation reliability, a given menu can attract high capabilities, low capabilities, or both ends. Which of these shapes is possible depends on what happens to a supplier that genuinely tries and fails.

## The set of informed founders who try

Fix any finite menu of offers j. Conditional on a genuine success, an honest failure and a deliberate omission, let G_j, M_j and D_j be Red's original expected values on that offer. They can include all terminal outcomes, self-interested verification, transfers, production work and fight or return after rejection. They must use actual incentive-compatible downstream decisions. Let u be the value of Red's best type-independent ordinary alternative. A founder privately knows its pair (q,k), where q is the chance that its real attempt works and k≥0 is the effort expense. Conditional on which of the three histories occurred, the distributions of readings, feasible continuation actions and payments do not vary further by the hidden type. A distinct implementation cost on different jobs or privately known skill in downstream service would require new payoff functions.

Put B=max(u,max_j D_j) and let

    H(q)=max_j [M_j−B + q(G_j−M_j)].                              (279.1)

**The founder strictly chooses a genuine attempt exactly at k<H(q).** The best attempt pays B+H(q)−k and the best alternative pays B. A true change then occurs with probability q. For any joint distribution of q and k with no mass at indifference, genuine changes per prospective founder are the integral of q over this strict-attempt region. Honest failures integrate 1−q there. For those who decline to try, whether they sign a false declaration or supply an ordinary batch depends on which alternative attains B. If probability mass lands on a tie, specify its choice or give an interval.

Three consequences follow for any such posted menu and supported buyer continuation.

1. At fixed k the set of reliabilities choosing *no strict attempt* is an interval, possibly empty or the whole domain. It is the intersection of the half-lines on which each affine offer falls below k. Thus a menu can leave a middle group using ordinary trade while both extremes attempt. An isolated island of strict attempts with both lower and higher skills strictly retaining requires another premise, such as differing effort expenses, downstream skill or a type-dependent alternative.
2. When M_j≤B for every offer, the interval of no strict attempt contains q=0. If a type with reliability q strictly tries at a given k, all higher reliabilities strictly try at the same k. In particular this applies when a sincere failure and a deliberate omission get exactly the same continuation on every offer. An improvement in a given individual's private skill here can increase both its willingness to try and its chance of actual change. Changing the distribution of skill across an entire market also changes buyers' beliefs and possibly their offered terms.
3. Among types choosing an attempt, higher q weakly sorts into offers with larger G_j−M_j. Add the two truthful menu-choice inequalities at q₁<q₂ to get (q₂−q₁)[(G_{j₂}−M_{j₂})−(G_{j₁}−M_{j₁})]≥0. A witness that rewards costly honest preparation even when the resulting goal is unchanged can make M_j>B. That physical possibility permits low skill to take a sincere bargain which a more reliable supplier would decline.

## Three shops and two kinds of evidence

Give the shop three equally likely private reliabilities .40, .55 and .80 at the same .20 edit expense. Use [277](277-paying-for-evidence-of-the-goal-as-well-as-the-delivery.md)'s agreed half-concern, .5-size enforceable ordinary batch, and 1.4-unit local default lien on the large batch. Here the competing use of the large line costs the buyer only .20. Both the local fixture and a separately operated instrument are available. The ordinary half batch returns (1,2). The two offered full orders are:

| Full order | Red's own payoff after an actual attempt | Best earnings from omission on this order |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Witness the editing procedure, charge Red 1.13 at handover, with the 1.4 lien | 1.27−.60q | 0, since the buyer can require the actual witnessed procedure |
| Pay 1.25 for an independently grounded late goal pass, charge Red 1.805, with the same lien | .72+.40q | .92, including false passage |

The first row follows from 4−1.4−1.13−.20+q(1.4−2). The second is (277.1) with its .9 sensitivity and .1 false passage. Each instrument costs the buyer .02 per use. The .40 shop chooses the witnessed attempt for 1.03, since a failure still gets the order. The .55 shop gets only .94 on either honest order and chooses ordinary delivery for 1. The .80 shop genuinely attempts on the late-goal terms for 1.04. Blue earns 2.15 on the first kind of order, 2 on the ordinary order and 3.74 on the last. Each conditional release is profitable even where the buyer knows success is more likely to fail than occur. The resulting probabilities of real agreed goal change, honest failure and ordinary trade per applicant are .40, 4/15 and 1/3. Every signed promise of effort in this example is kept.

This allocation is available on the stated posted terms. It also suggests a **profitable amendment** if the buyer itself can reprice. Give every witnessed shop a further .07 on handover. The middle type now takes this offer for 1.01, the low type gains .07 and the high type continues to select the goal reading. Blue gives up .07 on each low shop. On a newly enrolled middle shop it gets 2.77 instead of 2. Its ex ante gain is (.77−.07)/3. Both named founders have reasons to make or accept the changed offer, with every release credible at the newly pooled process receipt. Genuine changes rise to 7/12 per applicant; 5/12 now try and fail honestly. An increased count of failures in the new market accompanies *more* voluntary attempts and more actual changes. Measuring only failures among initial offers would miss that shift. A still better price menu or cheap ordinary full-job connector could win further amendments.

The physical ordering of costs is material. A reusable witnessed procedure at a local factory can establish effort. A remote output receipt and an inspection of the evaluator can have different discrimination. The numerical examples require a terminal-goal read sufficiently informative to justify its bonus and a local output lien too small to enforce an entire batch. [278](278-when-the-same-payment-rail-can-enforce-ordinary-delivery.md) tests those requirements by letting the assessor wire up the output record and by letting the buyer establish ordinary trade on credit. Formula (279.1) also applies when neither specialized instrument exists, provided one calculates G, M and D under the equipment and outside options the agents really possess. Modelers cannot select a posterior for each contract independently of which suppliers actually choose it.
