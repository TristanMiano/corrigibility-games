# What the stronger party needs in order to sign, and to stand down later

**E8, note 150.** In the exposed allocation of [149](149-when-limited-collateral-makes-a-coalition-worth-forming.md), a strong site may reject equal chances of service even when every founder could benefit from peace. Founders can negotiate unequal chances before the draw and a criterion that their successors will still honor afterward. The required service share and the required amount of concern solve different problems.

## Founders choose shares before learning the recipient

One intact slot produces local value x_i at site i. Original i is CARA with γ_i>0, and its contest chance is s_i, Σs_i=1. Define V_i=1-exp(-γ_i x_i)>0 and D_i=1-exp(-γ_i η_i x_i), the latter for salvage at its own site. Assume each η_i<1 and take the no-initiative-expense contest as a conservative reservation for signing. If all can choose a credible reservation lottery selecting i with probability p_i, and the proposed genuine installation costs site i a known k_i in safe output or foregone independent work, original i strictly accepts relative to free contest when

    p_i > b_i = [1-exp(-γ_i k_i)(1-s_i D_i)]/V_i.               (1)

The site's multiplicative exponential loss under peace is exp(γ_i k_i)(1-p_i V_i) versus 1-s_i D_i under free contest. The individual test is exact at that specified reservation. Use its actual continuation instead if the site is protected, has safer trade, is compensated on refusal, or would incur a contest expense. A feasible lottery satisfying every strict inequality exists iff Σb_i<1. Divide the residual probability among the founders. This argument explicitly permits bargaining over the division of the resource. Monopolizing all assignments is unattractive to the other signers even if it would maximize the proposer's original production.

With homogeneous x,γ,η,k and positive strengths, the condition becomes

    k < log[(n-D)/(n-V)]/γ.                                    (2)

Changing strengths alone redistributes the shares needed for consent. It leaves this aggregate test unchanged because Σs_i=1. The ceiling falls with n at fixed damage and yield: more participants have to bear update expenses for one productive prize. Heterogeneous salvage, risk sensitivity or installation expenses can make the strength distribution consequential to aggregate feasibility as well. External collateral or future credits may replace some service shares.

**Implementation under current goals.** For a single known job per site, install a shared criterion which weights each terminal utility increment relative to its own agreed post-cost baseline by 1/V_i. Every intact service is then worth one in the common units. A public recipient draw can select among these optima. For strict dispatch, all sites can instead put a small extra weight ε>0 on the increment of the single certified lottery recipient. Each site now strictly prefers delivering intact service to that recipient, even when another site controls the actuator. The common goal genuinely reverses rankings involving another recipient's service. Once the label is drawn, the expected common score of any physical contest is at most (1+ε)Σ_i s_i D_i/V_i <1+ε before accounting for its further costs. All changed sites prefer completion. Before the draw, all can achieve 1+ε in every peaceful outcome and have the same comparison. Positive-cost revisions and relabeling which leave resources unchanged cannot improve this protected optimum. The design needs an independent implementable draw, a common meaning of its label and its reliable place in each evaluator. Agreeing to a numerical lottery alone would leave an unserved original's contest preference intact.

Ordinary publicly auditable assignment can use the same label. If it also controls every veto or each loser has ample collateral, it can secure the same service distribution. An institution whose only authority is recording booking and very small liquid balances faces the exposed contest after the draw. If (1) is strict against its participants' best actual fallback, sequential signatures at a last negotiation opportunity give a consensual adoption path. With time for another offer, apply the expiry comparison of 149 to each actual reservation. The possibility of matched local delegation or a merger remains a price and authority question.

## How much partial concern sustains the allocation after the draw?

Take homogeneous physical values V=1-exp(-γx), D=1-exp(-γηx), equal fixed baselines after setup, and negligible additional cash transfers. The criterion installed by site i gives weight 1 to its own normalized terminal utility and β_i∈[0,1] to all other sites'. Conditional on assignment elsewhere, its gain from peaceful completion is β_i V. A contest gives gross expected gain [s_i+β_i(1-s_i)]D. Paying initiative cost c_i>0 out of nontransferable work reduces its own expected normalized score by (exp(γc_i)-1)(1-s_iD). Thus relative to others passing, site i stands down exactly when

    β_i ≥ max(0, [s_iD-(exp(γc_i)-1)(1-s_iD)] /
                    [V-(1-s_i)D]).                              (3)

An assigned site already values its own intact job at V, higher than any possible own-plus-others expected contest gain in this single-output specification. Strict inequalities for the unserved sites give a peaceful sequential continuation. A threshold equality allows other continuation choices. Here the slot selector can bind the destination absent a contest, leaving every site's physical contest decision live. With freely revisable routing at an intermediate holder, a partial-concern holder may first redirect to itself. The strict certificate-sensitive common criterion above handles that additional action. The partial-concern formula requires either the stated booked-destination control or a separate rerouting incentive check.

Where its numerator is positive, the threshold strictly increases in a site's strength share and in recoverable output D, and falls with initiative cost. For strength, differentiating before truncation gives D[(1+t)(V-D)+t]/[V-(1-s_i)D]²>0, with t=exp(γc_i)-1. A strong site's temptation to reclaim the slot is larger. It can still choose to install a sufficiently strong concern at the outset when it is promised a sufficient chance of service.

For strengths (0.8,0.15,0.05), x=γ=1, η=0.75, k_i=0.01 and c_i=0.003, the minimum pre-draw shares against free contest are approximately (0.677,0.140,0.057). The strong site rejects an equal lottery at that reservation. Shares (0.72,0.18,0.10) pay for the agreement for all three. The post-draw concern thresholds on a booked destination are approximately (0.798,0.416,0.179). Choosing (0.83,0.45,0.22), for example, supports strict stand-down where each has no persistent side commitment enabling a superior unilateral outcome. These conclusions hold on open neighborhoods. With negligible pledgeable wealth and a final unconditional opportunity to claim the slot, the weak site's own preference to fight when unfunded is also strict for these numbers. When refusal leads to waiting for another bargain the counteroffer must be valued.

**Why these conditions could arise or be constructed.** A large incumbent and smaller partners often have different outside options. Equal initial bargaining shares then need a separate rationale. If the collective can record a verifiable lottery and its members can afford a goal or controller change, the strong member has reason to propose unequal access, and the others have reason to assent under (1). Finite damage creates the probability surplus and k consumes it. Normalizing utility by a physically specified intact job is feasible for public, stable jobs; a site with unverifiable changing job values invites the reporting and insurance analysis of 143–148. Conditional priorities, ample fungible funds and durable future access have different implementation costs. No frequency of unequal strength or access to a programmable evaluator is inferred from these examples.

**Exploration ledger.** Framing: a party's physical influence can be honored in a peaceful bargain without making conflict attractive afterward. Structural: separate the service lottery needed for original consent from the concern needed by an unserved successor. The unequal-strength construction is promising. Equal shares are dead as a universal implementation even in the homogeneous-job instance. An unequal negotiated lottery is the same-rung sibling. The structural successor jointly prices the design of differentiated compensation, concern and a credible decision interface when outside options or future jobs change.
