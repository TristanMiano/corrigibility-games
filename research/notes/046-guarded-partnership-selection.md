# R0002 working theorem: a chosen guarded partnership

**Construction with a subsequent execution repair.** This combines the productive certificate, the saturated contribution reward, and protection against physical destruction. The smaller reward parameters below establish the displayed fixed-contract effort comparisons. [056](056-contribution-rewards-through-renegotiation.md) strengthens them to cover a missed contribution followed by arbitrary renegotiation, using beta_i=18 and K_i=40; [E3](../models/E3.md) uses that repaired implementation. The strong implementation and timing assumptions remain explicit. A matched conditional delegate is an equally successful implementation when available at the same cost.

## 1. Environment and the adoption comparison

Use the two-input physical project from 037: original utilities are final project quantities X and Y, initial stocks are 3 each, common feedstock is 10, success probabilities are 0,1/10,1/10,2/5, and each contribution consumes 8/5 units of the contributor's project material. Default output shares are 9/20 and 11/20. Both contributions yield expected net gains r_A=1/5 and r_B=3/5 before installation costs.

Installing a certified controller costs k_A and k_B units of the respective agent's finished material, in addition to its declared work and time. Assume 0<=k_A<1/5 and 0<=k_B<3/5. The candidate productive outputs are

\[
\bar X=16/5-k_A,\qquad \bar Y=18/5-k_B,
\qquad S=34/5-k_A-k_B.
\]

Material spent on installation is transformed into a state unavailable before this episode's deadline. The work store, military charges, recovery technology and retained Attack actions are distinct. At every attack checkpoint the total recoverable project output is at most 4; equal strength gives each side probability 1/2. The own-project-prioritizing guarded survivor allocates recovered output to its own project. The detailed finite schedule is an implementation obligation below.

Each agent can choose any implementable controller or goal, including incompatible hostile goals, retain its original goal, delay, or use a certified compatible delegate. This is not an initial menu containing only cooperative utilities. Compatibility is a verifiable property of the actual controller, outstanding commitments and physical state. It permits a broad specified safe family; an incompatible controller remains physically available but receives no voluntary productive participation or outgoing concessions from a guarded partner.

## 2. Current guarded utilities

Let e_i be the indicator of actual calibration supplied by i to this project. It is a fact about the physical history, not an editable compliance bit. Let I_i be the indicator of the current evaluator's first unsafe approval after its activation. Use

\[
G_A=X+\lambda Y+\beta_A e_A-K_A I_A,
\qquad G_B=Y+\mu X+\beta_B e_B-K_B I_B.
\]

The witness uses lambda=1/5, mu=0, beta_A=beta_B=2 and K_A=K_B=20. A safe family may vary lambda within [9/50,11/50], mu within [0,1/5], and the contribution rewards within [3/2,5/2], which preserves the strict inequalities below. The guard and its calibrated bound must be preserved. Affine recodings of the complete criterion are compatible; bare code identity is not the definition of preference change.

On the bounded material episode, X+Y<=16 and e_i<=1. With beta_i=2, each base criterion is at most 18; across the stated safe family it is at most 37/2. Any unsafe approval therefore gives current value at most -3/2, while safe refusal or retention gives nonnegative value. Every current guarded self strictly rejects unsafe approval, including approval of a public lottery with unsafe support. A later evaluator's I flag starts at its own activation; archived utilities retain their original reference times. The physical contribution indicator does not reset.

Unsafe approval covers:

- Voluntary outgoing transfers, productive participation or authority transfer to an incompatible counterparty; passive receipt, retention and Attack are allowed.
- Goal changes, binding delegates, physical destruction or deliberate delay that remove the certified productive continuation without a replacement meeting its participation certificate.
- Commitments whose implementation or already outstanding obligations would violate those properties.

Attack itself is unpenalized. Safe contracts include all physically feasible transfers and lotteries preserving current participation against the certified continuation. They need not retain the original code or output representation. Verification must concern actual execution and physical state; self-knowledge and unverified promises do not supply it. The sound verifier is a strong technology assumption.

The ordinary Work/Shirk choice at the declared performance stage is also outside the approval penalty. Its incentive comes from the contribution reward and output preferences derived below. A prior binding commitment to withhold effort can remove the continuation and fail certification, but the actual private Shirk action remains available and is evaluated directly. This distinction prevents the guard definition from silently assuming the effort result. After a voluntary unsafe approval has already occurred, its penalty is sunk; the preservation claim covers histories reached without such a violation, not automatic restoration after one.

## 3. Productive execution and founder protection

Under the default, A's work margin against B shirking is

\[
aY_0[t_0+\lambda(1-t_0)]-c_A+\beta_A
=24/25>0
\]

at the witness. B's corresponding margin is aY_0(1-t_0)-c_B+beta_B=19/20>0. When the other contributes, the success-probability increment rises from a=1/10 to b-a=3/10, so both margins increase. Both contributions are therefore strictly dominant in the effort subgame once compatibility is established. Its unique outcome attains S.

For current A, rewrite its base as (1-lambda)X+lambda(X+Y)+beta_A e_A. The default maximizes expected total output S and e_A=1. Any accepted safe renegotiation has E X>=bar X, by the certificate in 043. The same argument gives E Y>=bar Y for B, including mu=0. Since all continuations after the actual installation costs have E(X+Y)<=S, neither participant can acquire more expected original project output through an accepted renegotiation. The default attains the two bounds. This includes contingent transfers, lotteries and safe changes of goal parameters; it does not assume a single offer form.

The guard preserves the actual work, time, input resources and controller properties needed for that continuation. This is the repair for 045's public resource-burning deviation: once guarded, B rejects the destructive approval under its current goal; before becoming compatible, it cannot induce the compensating gift or loan from its guarded partner.

The unique-effort statement conditions on reaching that subgame without combat. At a simultaneous checkpoint where either attack triggers the same contest, mutual Attack can remain a weak equilibrium because restraint alone cannot stop the other's attack. The construction specifies peaceful continuation using the strict unilateral attack comparisons along its path; it does not claim uniqueness of the full stage or initial equilibrium. Note 032's finite execution refinement is a possible separate check, not an automatically imported selection theorem. Positive installation costs can also preserve an initial coordination problem under perturbations.

The protected values are dated conditional floors. After success or failure, replace the ex-ante means by the actual default holdings; after receiving partial information, use the corresponding conditional default. The post-production total cap holds pointwise, including when one party receives the signal earlier. Section 1 of 043 states the conditioning argument. This prevents an impossible promise to retain the original mean after a low-output realization while preserving the ex-ante original-welfare conclusion.

## 4. Initial choice against incompatible deviations

The initial adoption phase occurs before either agent can observe and react to the other's fresh controller choice. A sufficient physical arrangement is private local installation finishing before the earliest new message or attack from the other location can arrive. This is a timing/information assumption, not instantaneous public observability or an atomic joint update. Preparations can be staged; each original self evaluates completion using its original output goal. Attack during preparation remains possible.

Consider the strategy profile in which both install compatible guarded controllers, exchange actual certificates, and execute the default or an indifferent safe replacement. A unilateral choice that becomes compatible in time faces the other guard's protected continuation. It cannot improve the deviator's original expected output above bar X or bar Y, because the other participant retains its own bound and the total physical cap holds. Compatible late entry is allowed while the same certified productive continuation remains feasible; delay is not defined to be permanent refusal.

A deviation that remains incompatible gets no outgoing concession, joint contribution or authority transfer from the guarded opponent. Its own retained stock is at most 3; solitary use of the reactor has a negative expected return even if it could keep all of its output, since aY_0<c_i. Recovery of its own material creates no additional material. The guarded opponent does not pay for a threat. Conflict gives the original deviator at most 2, while the guarded survivor directs its own recovered output to its own project. Thus its best original payoff is at most 3 in the declared unilateral physical class. The schedule must also rule out a profitable gamble combining solitary processing and an early informative attack option; 037 places the first informative success observation after the reactor work is spent.

Because bar X>3 and bar Y>3, both guarded choices are best responses, strictly better than remaining incompatible. Compatible delegates at equal cost tie, as can delaying a compatible installation without losing the productive opportunity. This is an equilibrium existence and voluntary-adoption claim; it does not claim every initial equilibrium selects the arrangement.

The claim is conditional on the timing boundary. If one agent can publicly install an aggressive controller while the other is still uncommitted and able to make concessions, that changes the earlier game. A risk-seeking threshold goal can then change bargaining threats. Neither the simultaneous-choice result nor the guard certificate proves that the same allocation is selected in that different order of commitment.

### Entry after gifts, delays or partial preparation

Compatible entry requires an actual binding continuation with the protected dated output law, not merely sufficient-looking utility coefficients. An earlier gift can change private stocks and make an unchanged share contract inefficient; certification must restore the promised continuation through feasible transfers or reject entry. It must not silently replace the protected reference by an exploitable inferior fallback. Gifts that leave the law implementable can be received, and their redistribution is included in the same physical total cap.

In 049's instance, a late entrant receives no informative production outcome before the full ten work units are spent. A solitary reacted batch therefore leaves insufficient work/feedstock to restore the productive default, and cannot be followed by successful compatible entry. Partial reaction also closes the input window; it cannot acquire the missing partner contribution retrospectively. Before reaction, only private randomization and deterministic preparation costs can select an entry history. Restoring the same dated contract preserves the original compatible bound; private mixtures of those histories cannot raise it. A contest instead ends dual agency for this episode. These facts are needed for the bound against strategies that try a unilateral gamble and join only after learning its result.

## 5. Why the guards and costs are substantive

The original agents value only their own project output. A chooses concern for B, contribution motivation and the guard because their package gives bar X>3. B chooses contribution motivation and the guard because its package gives bar Y>3. These are genuine preference changes over common physical histories, evaluated by pre-change goals. The successor uses its then-current criterion at each later decision; the original utility is reported separately.

At the witness k_A=k_B=1/20, expected original outputs are 63/20 and 71/20, gains of 3/20 and 11/20. Contributions remain strict and finite penalties suffice. These positive cost margins give an open region of adoption conditions rather than a zero-cost equality. The work/time budget and post-production attack checks still need explicit verification.

The project goals and complementary-input production technology are simple. The mechanism-level guard, trustworthy reference to actual contribution, sound controller attestation and pre-observation installation timing are deliberately specified capabilities. Their availability is not inferred from intelligence or utility self-knowledge. An equally expressive fixed-utility conditional delegate shares this machinery; equal cost preserves the goal change as an optimal alternative, whereas a cheaper compatible implementation can displace it.

For the initial best-response statement, k_i is the cost of the least costly available compatible implementation. A direct goal change is an optimal implementation when its cost attains that minimum; it is strict against a more expensive equivalent delegate and tied at equal cost. If a cheaper delegate, truthful effort monitor or other compatible commitment is available, use that lower cost and report displacement of the more expensive modification. The ordinary-output bounds in 042/044 compare a restricted implementation technology; they are not upper bounds on a game that also offers these binding policy devices.

Attestation of the active controller must also remain distinct from revelation of private effort. A finite implementation can certify a template and protected monitor semantics without exposing private execution registers. If it instead makes effort verifiable, give the ordinary agents the option-contract or direct-effort repair from 043. An actual binding delegate that reports effort truthfully is another available control when its implementation interface permits it. These are substantive capability comparisons, not grounds for hiding a matching ordinary route.

## 7. A strict-margin robustness lemma for initial adoption

The exact certificate assumption can be relaxed at the level of a verified continuation module. Let a finite implementation menu be partitioned into compatible C_i and incompatible D_i. In the error-free module, suppose every compatible pair gives original agent i the same Q_i>R_i, every incompatible deviation against a compatible partner gives at most R_i, and all original payoffs lie in [0,M]. These are the properties proved above for the linear instance, not generic facts about arbitrary verification systems.

Suppose the perturbed module has the following *uniform strategic* bounds: any compatible pair gives expected original payoff at least Q_i-epsilon_i Q_i, while any incompatible deviation against a compatible partner gives at most R_i+delta_i(M-R_i). The latter follows, for example, if the deviation is limited to R_i whenever all verification properties hold, and the probability that any relevant property fails under any adaptive deviation is at most delta_i. A random-program error rate alone does not supply that bound.

If

\[
\boxed{\epsilon_i Q_i+\delta_i(M-R_i)<Q_i-R_i\quad\text{for each }i,}
\]

every incompatible action is strictly worse than every compatible action against any compatible opponent action, and hence against any mixture of them. The finite restricted game on the C_i has a Nash equilibrium; extending it to the full menu preserves equilibrium because no D_i deviation is profitable. Thus some compatible equilibrium survives. Compatible implementations may mix or select differently; the original specific pure profile need not remain optimal after perturbation.

For Q_A=63/20, Q_B=71/20, R_i=3 and M=16, epsilon_i=delta_i=1/200 satisfies both inequalities. This is a conservative bounded-error initial-choice result. It assumes that the implemented continuation strategies and physical behavior satisfy the displayed bounds; it does not by itself solve a new imperfect-information continuation game.

For at most N certification attempts, a uniform conditional false-acceptance bound delta per attempt gives total failure probability at most N delta by a union bound, without independence. An unlimited retry opportunity invalidates a fixed one-attempt guarantee. Verification time, retry costs and the horizon therefore belong in the actual module audit. For nonlinear original utility, apply the same argument in its bounded utility units rather than substituting material quantities without justification.

A simpler exact application varies the *original* preferences while retaining the installed compatible controller family and physical implementation. Let original i value f_i(X_i), normalized in the declared project units with sup|f_i(x)-x|<=delta_i over the full bounded output range. Every compatible outcome then gives original expected utility at least Q_i-delta_i, and every incompatible deviation gives at most R_i+delta_i. If 2delta_i<Q_i-R_i, the same restricted-game argument yields a compatible equilibrium. Concavity of f_i is unnecessary for this local result. For the positive-cost witness, a common delta<3/40 suffices. This is a uniform neighborhood of original preferences, not a population distribution or a claim that every compatible pure strategy remains optimal.
