# Assessment audit and the scope of continued agreement

**R0004 mathematical audit; self-review.** The following distinctions are
part of the theorem premises, not conclusions supplied by a small tree.

## 1. Current-self sequential rationality

The finite checker treats each installed evaluator as the criterion at
its actual decision nodes. Before installation it evaluates every branch
with the incumbent criterion, including the successor's ensuing best
responses. The same original sender may act at several earlier nodes;
perfect recall and the finite one-shot deviation argument then justify
checking every such information set. It does not permit an incumbent to
override a successor's utility while evaluating a deviation.

The common perturbation supplies all information-set beliefs at once.
The checker asserts identical actions, current evaluators and behavioral
probabilities at nodes grouped into one information set. It computes
conditional action losses using the leading path probabilities, rather
than ignoring unreachable decisions. These checks cover the declared
tree. The new-program price bounds and initial custody device are
additional analytical premises.

The continuous-policy envelope minimizes the actual quadratic at each
receiver information set: y=clip(P(high)+a,0,1). Its verification covers
every policy in [0,1] when sender attack is dominated. It is not an
envelope for the threshold-hostility case, whose discontinuity needs its
own separate comparison. The paid low quote has the explicit concave
profit function in 080. R spends its quote expense on sending the quote;
S spends its execution expense only on acceptance. Rejection does not
refund R's expense. Both costs occur in the displayed on-path welfare.

The stochastic kernel has ideal configured randomization as a primitive,
just as it has a configured combat win probability. The finite rational
goal bank removes the need to install an irrational utility coefficient.
It does not by itself prove an engineering implementation of arbitrary
real priors, exact mixed probabilities or arbitrarily precise attestation.
Finite-precision approximate equilibrium is a further implementation
question; exact rational fixtures and the ideal parameter theorem are
kept distinct from that claim.

## 2. Initial choice, individual continuation and renegotiation

At a peaceful checkpoint i, let H_i>0 be the high original sender's
remaining expected expense above receiving policy 1 immediately. The low
sender's remaining value above policy a is e_{i-1}, while receiving 1
would give g. Thus switching immediately to policy 1 offers sender gains
H_i when high and g-e_{i-1} when low. R is indifferent between policy 1
and the equilibrium checking mixture at the current posterior.

Suppose a *binding current-execution agreement* can implement this switch
with sender expense J_S, receiver expense J_R, and a funded payment tau
from S. At this fixed posterior it is strictly preferred by R and by
both sender types whenever

\[
J_R<\tau<\min\{H_i,g-e_{i-1}\}-J_S,
\qquad \tau\le\delta.
\tag{1}
\]

This proves a conditional interim improvement, not an equilibrium of a
new recurrent offer game. A proposal or rejection can change beliefs;
earlier low claiming changes when agents anticipate a new settlement;
and an uncommitted receiver's fallback need not be the original checking
mixture. In particular, when rejection itself leads R to choose policy 1,
the sender will reject a positive price for the same policy. One cannot
infer unraveling merely from comparing payoffs while freezing beliefs.

In the full-budget package delta=0, so a strictly positive payment in
(1) is unavailable. The initial payment has really left, and return
transport cannot finish in the episode. In 080's disagreement, money is
released only after refusal. Allowing other escrow release rules, loans,
new counterparties or reusable settlement windows changes those premises.
They require a new proposal/continuation analysis, searching both for
successful compromise and for surviving obstructions.

Hence the established level is selected sequential equilibrium and
individual current-goal continuation in the stated interface. Coalition
stability, renegotiation-proofness and long-run prevalence are not inferred.
Nor does the existence of (1) erase the actual initial and continuation
results already established for the specified game.

## 3. What a fully fixed-utility match requires

The tree's D control replaces the sender's last genuine update with a
protected truthful report. It preserves that sender's original utility;
the receiver still has its declared current goal. Reproducing the entire
two-party goal package with fixed original utilities additionally requires
binding receiver policy authority implementing the negotiated rule.

If the state protection and policy authority are actually available at
the same total expense, the fully fixed implementation reproduces the
same outcomes and original welfare. If it is cheaper it can displace the
corresponding goal implementation; at equal cost genuine change can tie
as an optimal voluntary choice. The ordinary bounds deliberately grant
strong receiver policy/launch commitment, but withhold protected sender
state evidence except for the specified contest. These are different
controls, not interchangeable meanings of knowing one's own utility.

## 4. Finite stocks, physical attacks and initial completion

The no-loot and payment-cap assumptions are substantive. Each party's
20 units are specialized project components; the stated time/work budget
does not convert the counterparty's components into its own project
output. Remote cash is separately transferable under the custody rules.
Destruction after a captured lie can remove at most the remaining stock.
An extra goal does not create cash or override those conversion limits.
If capture permits useful confiscation or longer-run conversion, replace
the cost/cap law and rederive the comparison.

The early and field price bounds in 084 cover effective authority,
including partial installation, and compare only remaining expenses at
later histories. Initial attacks lose finite output; they cannot seize
the remote funded settlement. The old receiver's strict initial
participation implies completion after its first installment is sunk.
After installation it uses the new criterion. These are sufficient
current-self checks, not an assertion that arbitrary modification is
atomic, irreversible or semantically transparent.
