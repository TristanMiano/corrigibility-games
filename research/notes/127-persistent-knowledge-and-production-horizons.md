# Persistent knowledge, production horizons and finite research opportunities

R0006 mathematical extension of the additive shared-knowledge class. This
distinguishes research performed once from note 119's recurring maintenance.
It gives both a region where a longer horizon increases the benefit of
consensual change and a region where original incentives catch up. The
comparison is in original own-color output; adoption still needs an actual
launch and comparison with the best available ordinary implementation.

## 1. A one-time investment with a finite useful scope

Let each agent perform research `x_i in [0,E]` once, at useful-work expense
`c x_i^2/2`, and receive its productive services over a duration L>0. The
same knowledge is reusable throughout that interval. With fresh material
and usable-energy flows sufficient for the stated production, final counts
are

`q_i=Q(L)+La x_i+L rho a x_j-(c/2)x_i^2-K_i`,             (1)

where a,c,E>0, 0<=rho<=1, and K_i is the physical own-output opportunity
cost of goal installation. Q(L) includes baseline production; the initial
budget separately funds research and installation. L can be a continuous
operating duration or an integer number of identical production periods.
A discounted annuity interpretation changes the criterion to discounted
own-color counts and must be labeled rather than assumed for the original
undiscounted maximizers. This note uses undiscounted counts.

The finite cap E represents the research questions or usable improvements
available in this episode. There is no unmodeled sequence of new research
projects. Future innovation that expands E is an explicit extension below.
Research output is knowledge; c x_i^2/2 is work consumed obtaining it, not
an entropy identity. Flow energy and raw inputs still bound actual clips.

The original effort and the symmetric total-maximizing effort are

`x_F=min{La/c,E}`, `x_C=min{La(1+rho)/c,E}`.               (2)

Each follows by maximizing a strictly concave own-effort quadratic. Shared
goals q_B+q_R make x_C the unique common-payoff effort equilibrium. When
there is an available choice between colors, a balanced common goal as in
119 protects the negotiated split. Private finishing is the baseline here.

Define y=La/(cE), and let Delta(L) be each founder's gross gain from both
using x_C rather than x_F, before installation expense. Direct subtraction
gives the exact classification

`Delta/(cE^2) = rho^2 y^2/2`,                  if `0<=y<=1/(1+rho)`;
`                (1-y)[(1+2rho)y-1]/2`,       if `1/(1+rho)<=y<=1`;
`                0`,                         if `y>=1`.              (3)

At rho=0 all three portions give zero on their applicable domains. For
rho>0, Delta is strictly positive on 0<y<1. The pieces agree at the two
boundaries, and their first derivatives agree at y=1/(1+rho). On the middle
interval the derivative is `1+rho-(1+2rho)y`, so the unique maximum is

`y_peak=(1+rho)/(1+2rho)`,
`Delta_max=cE^2 rho^2/[2(1+2rho)]`.                       (4)

Consequently increasing duration raises this reciprocal productive gain
up to y_peak, then lowers it until the originals independently choose E.
This last region has high output and full research by unchanged maximizers.
The declining incremental gain is not a decline in total production or a
rise in combat, and it says nothing about preference change for different
future opportunities.

## 2. Exact positive-cost participation interval

Suppose both installations cost K>0, and the selected conditional agreement
would implement x_C. Strict initial participation relative to x_F requires
K<Delta(L). Put `k=2K/(cE^2)`. The interval is empty for
`k>=rho^2/(1+2rho)`. Below that maximum, the upper endpoint is

`y_+=[1+rho+sqrt(rho^2-(1+2rho)k)]/(1+2rho)`.             (5)

The lower endpoint is

`y_-=sqrt(k)/rho` if `k<=rho^2/(1+rho)^2`,
`y_-=[1+rho-sqrt(rho^2-(1+2rho)k)]/(1+2rho)` otherwise.   (6)

Both founders strictly prefer the package exactly for y_-<y<y_+ in this
comparison. These formulas come from solving the two quadratics in (3),
using the lower branch appropriate to the join point. Asymmetric expenses
replace K by max(K_B,K_R) for balanced no-transfer terms; feasible transfers
or a different common goal can enlarge that particular interval.

For a=c=E=rho=1, the gross gain peaks at L=2/3 with value 1/6. At K=1/8,
strict balanced participation occurs for `1/2<L<5/6`. At L>=1 both originals
already choose the useful research cap; an additional positive-cost goal
change serving only this task is displaced by retaining their goals.
The continuous duration normalization can be rescaled to arbitrary years
or periods without changing the dimensionless boundaries.

Increasing rho at a fixed L weakly raises Delta. In the unsaturated region
its derivative is cE^2 rho y^2; in the partially saturated region it is
cE^2 y(1-y); beyond y=1 the derivative is zero. Thus greater useful sharing
expands this productive adoption opportunity even though increasing the
horizon can eventually shrink it. Different parameter changes answer
different causal questions.

## 3. Paying to expand the useful research scope

To test the finite-cap limitation, allow each agent to build research
capacity E_i at physical cost kappa E_i^2/2, then choose x_i<=E_i. Assume
that this construction really supplies new usable research opportunities,
not merely empty laboratory space, and that finite budgets permit the
optima below. Capacity has no other benefit or resale value. For every
positive selected x_i the least-cost capacity is E_i=x_i. Substitution
replaces c by c+kappa in the complete optimization, for both originals
and common-goal agents. Thus

`x_F=La/(c+kappa)`, `x_C=La(1+rho)/(c+kappa)`,
`Delta_expand=(La rho)^2/[2(c+kappa)]`.                    (7)

There is no saturation-induced reversal inside this interior region.
The agents themselves pay for and choose the greater capacity under the
common criterion; its cost is included in (7). Initial originals prefer
the combined goal-and-capacity package when its net gain exceeds the best
actual alternative, including an ordinary binding capacity/effort plan.
This is a chosen-investment result within the specified joint control
technology. It does not establish that valuable new questions are always
available for purchase. A hard remaining resource cap restores a piecewise
boundary like (3).

The causal distinction is therefore explicit: fixed research scope makes
long duration eventually supply sufficient private incentives; expandable
scope can preserve the benefit of reciprocal accommodation. Both involve
exclusive original final goals and reusable shared knowledge. Neither is
a population prevalence claim.

## 4. Retained conflict, revisions and ordinary controls

The formulas are productive continuation values. They are not a theorem
that a long-lived original remains peaceful. At each date, a fight can
capture knowledge, material, converters or remaining production time; its
actual recovery and delay must be compared with the remaining counts.
Note 115 supplies a finite-episode sufficient peace embedding, but a bound
proved for one duration cannot silently be reused for arbitrarily large L.
Longer time can improve repair as well as productive returns. The same
qualification applies to an attack by an already modified successor using
its current common goal.

Within the stated fixed opportunity set, achieving a common global maximum
rules out profitable goal rollback. New research opportunities, alternative
partners or an improved capture technology change that set and require a
new comparison. Current-original voluntary adoption, selected execution
and global future stability remain distinct claims.

An enforceable ordinary investment plan reaches the same x_C at matched
cost. A merely revocable promise returns to the own-effort optimum (2).
If binding ordinary implementation is cheaper, it wins the initial
comparison; if genuine installation is a least-cost actual route, it can
be selected, including at a tie. The finite-scope result gives a substantive
ordinary success and the expandable-scope result preserves the constructive
goal-change opportunity without imposing a preference for either answer.
