# R0002 comparison: implementation costs, collateral and equilibrium scope

**Derived comparison of dated productive frontiers.** A goal change can be a beneficial implementation while ordinary contracts become more competitive as collateral grows. This note puts their actual costs into the comparison and states precisely which equilibrium conclusions the guarded construction supports.

## 1. The extra implementation cost must be smaller than the incentive saving

Use 044's symmetric linear technology. Its full-effort surplus before implementation is C=4/5. Write the exact ordinary mediated frontier as N_o(z), where z is effective collateral wealth, and define its incentive loss

\[
g(z)=C-N_o(z)=
\begin{cases}
2/5,&3\le z\le71/10,\\
7/(5z-18),&z>71/10.
\end{cases}
\]

Suppose the guarded implementation costs a total k_g in finished material and ordinary mediation costs a total k_o, split equally between the agents. A required minimum final holding m leaves ordinary effective collateral z=R-k_o/2-m. Assume z>=3 and that the guarded full-effort contract satisfies its own collateral and reserve constraints. Measured from the same original initial stocks, the two total net gains are

\[
N_g=C-k_g,\qquad N_o^{net}=N_o(z)-k_o.
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{N_g-N_o^{net}=g(z)-(k_g-k_o).}
\]

The guarded productive frontier is strictly larger exactly when its extra implementation cost is less than the ordinary incentive loss. Equality gives equal total output; a larger extra cost favors the ordinary productive frontier. This is an exact comparison of attainable effort/transfer totals in the stated classes. An individual adoption claim also needs shares, participation and actual execution. In particular, 044's privately mediated mechanism still needs its earlier-stage combat audit before being called a complete peaceful equilibrium.

For k_g=1/10, k_o=m=0, the guarded gain is 7/10. At R=3 the ordinary gain is 2/5, so the net advantage is 3/10. At R=10 the ordinary gain is 93/160, giving advantage 19/160. The frontiers tie at

\[
R=88/5=17.6.
\]

Above that stock, the zero-cost ordinary mediated frontier exceeds the positive-cost guarded frontier. This is the constructive force of collateral: stronger feasible success/failure incentives and private correlation can reduce the material cost of inducing effort without changing the original utility. A positive reserve m shifts the comparison through usable collateral, while a more expensive ordinary mediator shifts both its material cost and its collateral.

The costless ordinary mechanism is a deliberately favorable upper-bound control when real mediation consumes resources. It is not an assertion that private mediation is physically free. Conversely, the guard's declared costs are charged rather than waived. If a binding delegate supplies the same complete guarded behavior at lower cost, that delegate can displace direct modification; equal cost permits a tie.

## 2. Why a supported adoption equilibrium is distinct from the joint frontier

The guarded choice game asks whether installing a compatible package is a best response when the partner installs its compatible package. The package includes a protected default contract and an incompatible-partner gate. A unilateral incompatible choice cannot induce a concession from that already guarded partner; this supplies the relevant deviation bound. Different compatible shares can therefore support different initial equilibria.

The joint frontier instead asks what the original pair could arrange if both chose a different implementation. At sufficiently large collateral, a cheaper ordinary mechanism can offer a higher aggregate original payoff even while the more costly guarded profile remains a coordination equilibrium. A frontier advantage should not be silently converted into unique selection, and an inferior joint frontier should not be hidden behind a unilateral best-response statement. Which package agents propose and coordinate on is a further bargaining question.

For the main small-stock witness, this ambiguity does not erase the productive comparison: the guarded net surplus 7/10 exceeds even the enlarged costless ordinary output-transfer bound 2/5. It also does not establish unique preference modification, because an equally capable matched delegate can implement the same productive continuation.

## 3. Current-self and information scope

At initial installation the original criterion evaluates the actual continuation. Once a different goal activates, that evaluator governs its subsequent decisions and scores later replacements by its own history criterion. An archived evaluator has no authority over a successor. The current-self convention follows 018/029, including their distinction between an evaluator epoch and a code label.

E3's initial-choice proposition is a conditional Nash best-response result given its specified compatible continuation and uniform deviation bounds. The numerical construction supplies original adoption, the fixed-contract effort calculation strengthened by 056's all-continuation missed-obligation bound, safe-renegotiation participation, and unilateral attack checks at its reached physical histories. It permits weak selection among compatible implementations and peaceful continuations.

This is not a proof of a unique sequential equilibrium of every expanded private-program bargaining game. Such a claim would need the full protocol, information sets, off-path belief system and strategy continuation after every incompatible controller. The public-state existence theorem in 018/029 must not be imported to hidden effort or private implementation types without that work. The initial uniform original-payoff bounds do cover arbitrary incompatible behavior, but a payoff bound and a complete off-path equilibrium construction are different objects.

For a finite implementation library one can specify all those extra choices and seek a sequential extension; no semantic oracle or infinite-menu existence theorem follows from the present finite witnesses. Keep the achieved conditional adoption and execution conclusions, while leaving that stronger completion question explicit. The public-conversion theorem in 054 similarly states a necessary outcome restriction on sequentially rational equilibria of its declared module, not existence for every unspecified bargaining protocol.

## 4. A targeted next comparison

A useful extension would let the original pair select among productive goal change, private mediation and binding delegation while paying their actual setup costs, then allow new partners or opportunities. It should preserve the small-stock successful construction and test how the cost/collateral boundary changes the selected implementation. That investigates both adopted accommodation and effective unchanged-goal cooperation, rather than treating every rejection of modification as combat.
