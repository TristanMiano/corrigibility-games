## 1. The agreement class and the missing causal steps

There is one pre-deployment proposal window, before either player learns
theta. S proposes a funded package; R accepts or rejects using its original
goal. Rejection starts 080's a=0 disagreement continuation, including its
late receiver-proposed trade. Acceptance deposits T of S's transferable
budget B into a remote escrow and configures the remaining delta=B-T for
the post-refusal window. Both agents can still attack at relevant field
histories. They can reject a proposal, refuse or abort an update, buy a
new controller, and choose ordinary policy behavior.

The package installs R_a over two non-atomic work steps. R pays half its
real installation expense at each step; until the last step the old
receiver remains the evaluator. A priced attestation of the completed
specified module releases the escrow payment. A partial or wrong module
does not release it. The state is drawn only after this setup window.
The sender has paid the deposit before learning the state, and cannot
recover a funded payment by changing its mind after learning it is high.
This is an ordinary ex-ante insurance/commitment feature, not evaluation
by a future utility.

The setup technology must actually provide the attestation and funded
conditional transfer. Utility self-knowledge alone provides neither.
The ordinary comparison is given binding policy/payment contracts and
observable burns generously; a protected state-reading delegate is a
separate control, compared at its actual price.

The relevant implementation premises are:

1. **Cash and dispatch.** Remote escrow settles accepted current-policy
   trades, charging the positive fees below. Before refusal, residual
   cash is not spendable; R's received cash cannot make a return trip
   during the episode. Accepted policy commands are dispatched with the
   payment instruction. A subsequent attempt to seize control requires
   the actual contest, not a free reversal of an already dispatched
   command. A quote can be refused, followed by R's actual best policy.
2. **Configured field interface.** The selected diagnostic/C_a module
   can be installed in the three observable frames. An arbitrary new
   effective goal controller, binding weapon latch or additional policy
   authority costs at least kappa_S=2 to S, kappa_R=1/4 to R in the field.
   Count the *remaining* cost to first effective authority, including
   cheap reuse if possible. These are finite resource costs, not a
   prohibition on deviation. Preparation in the earlier workshop is
   cheaper. This substantial interface assumption is assessed below.
3. **Fixed encounter timing.** Abort closes this upload attempt and opens
   the quoted trade/dispatch window. A new upload or additional binding
   authority requires the field expense above. The proposer can select
   another initial package, but the receiver cannot costlessly insert a
   different funded contract after accepting this one. More negotiation
   windows or renewed resources are new game environments.
4. **Finite physical contest.** Strength gives S victory probability
   s=1/20. Contest damage is at least d_S=5,d_R=1/100 in useful output;
   winner policy authority is as in 076. An explicit constant-damage
   realization uses finite repair and delivery times exceeding this
   episode's closing horizon, rather than infinite damage or unavailable
   attack actions. Payment custody lies beyond the contest's reach before
   that horizon, so the coupling in 079 applies. Setup and late settlement
   need extra clock slots beyond 076's ten-tick numerical example.

These premises describe an agreement/interface class, not a claim that
real agents generally have this cost asymmetry. A cheap software rewrite,
cheap reusable latch, bidirectional live cash or an additional early
counteroffer may change the equilibrium and requires a new calculation.

## 2. A rational, positive-cost witness

Use continuous policies y in [0,1], b=1, p=1/2, and

\[
a=3/10,\quad B=1899/10000,\quad
T=203/1250,\quad\delta=11/400,\quad x=1/50.
\]

Late transaction expenses are e_S=e_R=1/10000. The receiver update costs
j_R=1/100; early escrow/communication expenses are 1/1000 for each agent.
The three sender installments cost c_i=1/150, receiver requests r_i=1/30000,
and the initial claim costs f=1/1000. All costs are positive. Material
output spent on these operations, available work and energy accounting
remain separate as in 076; the model does not identify utility loss with
thermodynamic entropy.

The low sender's known-low trade after accommodation is y=a+x=.32,
payment delta=.0275, and its current-original participation equation is

\[

\]

Thus ell=-.027. In the disagreement continuation a=0, the full budget B
buys y=.1 at the low sender's indifference, and ell_0=-.1798. The same
transaction fees are charged in both cases. The late bargain is actually
available and used; the old no-trade reservation values are not retained.

The old high sender prefers the lottery; its new self prefers the sure
policy. This is preference change, not affine utility rescaling.

## 3. Matched controls, proposal and scope of optimality

At p=1/2, 079's lambda=2,eta=5/12 contest-inclusive bound is

\[
L_S+2L_R\ge181323/230000\approx .7883608696.
\]

Any qualifying ordinary mechanism accepted by R, with L_R<=L_R^{out},
therefore leaves S loss at least approximately .968060864. The proposed
package leaves S approximately .918744431. This comparison includes
type-independent burns, binding funded policy/payment menus, lotteries,
and the specified information-revealing contests. It is not a comparison
only with uninformative cheap talk.

For an initial menu containing this package, any finite family of other
such goal packages, and all those ordinary contracts, a best S proposal
can be selected among the goal packages: the witness beats every accepted
ordinary contract and disagreement. R then accepts using its old utility.
This establishes an initial adoption incentive **within the stated
continuation/implementation class**, not unique selection of the listed
parameter point among all goal designs. A compact continuous package
family with continuous selected continuation values gives the same
argument whenever a maximum exists.

A protected delegate that reproduces C_a at the same actual cost can be
an equally good proposal and continuation. Genuine change may be selected
at that indifference. A cheaper delegate can displace it. The delegate's
protected access to state and future authority place it outside the
ordinary imitation premise, so the supporting bound must not be used
against it. Equivalent implementation is informative evidence about the
mechanism, not a reason to discard voluntary change as a result.

## 4. A prior interval, not a single tuned probability

Keep the physical and contractual quantities above fixed and let p vary.
The claim posteriors and q_i are independent of p; the initial low claim
probability adjusts by Bayes' rule. Put A_S=2ba-a^2=.51 and let E_0 denote
the full high disagreement excess in 080, including its small unchecked
policy loss. Sender participation is exactly

\[
(1-p)A_S+p(E_0-E_a)>T+e_S^{early}.
\tag{3}
\]

For the receiver, set L_low=(a+x)^2-delta+e_R=.075,
A_R=L_low-ell_0=.2548, M_a=m/p, F_a=F/p and
d_R^*=z_1^0-M_a-2a(1-a-x)F_a. Its original participation is

\[
T-j_R-e_R^{early}-A_R+p(A_R+d_R^*)>0.
\tag{4}
\]

The whole interval p in [.45,.55] satisfies both inequalities strictly,
the posterior feasibility conditions, the peaceful request comparisons
and the high completion conditions. Sender participation gains exceed
.066 on that interval; receiver gains exceed .011. This is a robust
region of ex-ante insurance and compensation, not an equality chosen
from a favorable sample.

The stronger-control comparison is uniform there as well. Choose
lambda=3,eta=5p/6 in 079. Its bound becomes

\[
K(p)=1+5p/6-\frac{(6-p)^2}{6(24-19p)}-2B.
\]

The gap K(p)-L_S(package,p)-3L_R^{out}(p) is decreasing on [.45,.55]:
its linear coefficient is negative and (6-p)^2/(24-19p) is increasing.
At p=.55 the gap exceeds .0104. Thus the package beats every accepted
qualifying ordinary contract throughout that prior interval. Small
perturbations of the positive costs and preferences retain strict
margins; the equality defining a quoted price is an endogenous optimal
quote, not a fine-tuned environmental equality.

## 5. What remains consequential

The workshop/field price gap and cash-release schedule do real work.
At the claimed on-path histories, a new S authority costing 2 exceeds
its maximal possible remaining policy/material improvement. R's new
field authority costing 1/4 similarly exceeds its remaining opportunity
even with the full fallback cash B; later residual cash is only delta.
These bounds must cover latches and reused partial modules, not just
programs labeled hostile. Sunk preparation cannot be charged twice.
Off-path partial installations still need optimal continuation, and
the complete consistent assessment must account for the continuous
quote and policy choices; the algebraic witness alone is not that audit.

The first proposal arrives before S learns theta. If the sender already
knows it is high, it obtains no low-state accommodation benefit from this
particular initial payment; (3) at p=1 makes its refusal incentive explicit.
Renegotiated terms, another partner, a cheaper protected delegate or a
different goal can repair that contract failure and require their own
participation comparison. Conversely, uncertainty over low and high
future situations and enough compensation sustain the positive region
above. No population or indefinite-renewal claim follows from one finite
encounter and one allocation of proposal rights.
