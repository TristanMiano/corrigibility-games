# How much evidence should a coalition require before activating its new goals?

**E8, note 161.** Unanimous consent and unanimously positive technical readings are different requirements. Insisting on both can reject so many real installations that an individual would rather keep its old goal and hope to receive its partners' services. In the referral ring the founders can price this risk and choose a rule for interpreting their equipment. The ordinary comparison includes both a credible matched controller and a false claim that lacks its behavior.

## The signed operation and its actual payoffs

As in 153 and 157, exactly one edge of a homogeneous n-ring is selected uniformly after negotiation, the donor forgoes d and its recipient gains r>d. All founders must expressly authorize a particular offer. If any declines, nobody else's goal is changed. Signers can configure a finite recognized common-goal template, paying K from their original work reserve, or falsely claim to have configured it at zero update expense. A sincere conditional template retains the original ranking if the authorized gate stays closed; after it opens the donor strictly prefers the valuable referral. A false claimant retains its own objective and keeps its job if called as donor. Ordinary binding delegation which actually delivers the same service is a separate honest control, priced with its true hardware and evidence costs.

Suppose the inspection returns a positive reading with probability t on a genuine effective configuration and m<t on a false one. Signals are independent given these controller types and supplied before the productive edge is known. Each participant sees the agreed decision rule and the resulting public gate. Readback here means testing a bounded configured objective and its active execution path. Testing arbitrary motives from observed cooperative behavior alone cannot justify the given rates. False negatives can arise from ambiguous but genuine records, false positives from an insufficiently informative measurement. Multiple checks sharing the very same physical failure mode must be priced with their actual joint law. Charge a real resource cost h for obtaining the readings in addition to K. A funded observer can be paid before the readings; merely promising discretionary inspection afterward is a different game.

Write T for the probability the gate opens if all are sincere and D_i for the probability it opens if only i fakes. On a successful honest path each founder obtains gross g=(r-d)/n. A fake can receive r/n from its sincere predecessor and incurs no donor loss. Write H=r/n. The exact unilateral strict condition is

    gT-H D_i > K  for every i.                                  (1)

Open refusal gives zero before sunk common inspection fees. If accepted, the gate really controls all nominated updates. Actual common-goal activation then occurs with probability T, producing n changes, full referral and zero combat in this short-expiry example. On honest technical rejection the costly preparatory work has been spent but actual common goals remain dormant and independent sites keep their jobs. Genuine preparation, successful activation and counted productive peace are different measurements. Fees are individually worth authorizing on the indicated all-sincere equilibrium when gT-K-h exceeds their actual alternatives. A binding ordinary club at lower all-in cost can change which legitimate device is selected.

## Designing the activation rule

With identical sites any symmetric acceptance rule is a probability a_j of opening after j of n positive readings. Permuting arbitrary site labels privately in the rule preserves T, averages D_i and cannot worsen the maximum unilateral deviation. A public permutation revealed early can create different information. Let P_j be the binomial probability of j given all sincere. Relative to that law, the probability of j readings when one site is false has likelihood ratio

    L_j = (j/n)m/t + (1-j/n)(1-m)/(1-t).                         (2)

Condition on whether the false claimant's location in an all-sincere count j is one of the j positives. Thus T=ΣP_j a_j, D=ΣP_j L_j a_j. The ratio decreases with j. The largest attainable truth incentive before K is Σ_j P_j (g-H L_j)_+. A fee K at or above this bound cannot yield *strict* genuine installation by changing the count-based gate alone. For maximizing actual activation subject to (1), accept the count classes in order of decreasing j, possibly randomizing at one boundary. To prove it, exchanging equal all-sincere probability mass from a lower accepted j to a higher unaccepted j preserves T and lowers D. Equivalently minimize D for each given T by this exchange. A strict incentive can be implemented arbitrarily close to a boundary by leaving slack. The statistics are an ordinary binary-decision likelihood-ratio construction; the payoffs and the relevant T-versus-D tradeoff here come from genuine donor and recipient choices.

Example with n=3, r=6, d=1, t=0.5, m=0.05, K=0.22. Requiring three positive readings gives T=0.125, D=0.0125 and gT-HD≈0.18333. A false claim is profitable. Requiring two gives T=0.5, D=0.275 and a margin 0.28333>K. Accepting two or more and additionally 16% of the one-positive profiles gives T=0.56 and D=0.353, with gross incentive margin about 0.22733>K. The latter rule still requires three signatures and three actual target configurations on the genuine path. If h=0.02, its original expected net return is about 0.69333 per site, compared to zero from independent service. The signers can afford and choose this probe and rule. For sufficiently high h the worthwhile operational change can still be too expensive to launch. A local rule that asks for positive readings only at sender and recipient has honest delivery probability t² and a single fake's chance of receiving mt. Its distinct incentive margin is [t²(r-d)-mt r]/n, which can support a stronger anti-fake incentive but less actual service in this example. Edge-conditional goals and full-group activation are distinct adoption mechanisms.

## A stronger test against collective replacements

In the unanimity-of-readings case T=t^n. Consider a proper subset S of l sites that coordinates replacement controllers and internal transfers at zero cost. Let b be the number of ring edges from outsiders into the subset, equal to its number of outward boundary edges. The subset has l-b internal edges. Give its deviators, as a generous comparison, all possible internal productive surplus even if the public gate never opens, and allow them to refuse every outward service. This grants resources for internal coalition control, not the ability to bind nonmembers. If all l replacement controllers have the false reading law, outsiders are activated with probability D_l=t^(n-l)m^l. The maximum aggregate original payoff of this deviation is bounded by

    [(l-b)(r-d)+b D_l r]/n.                                    (3)

Only an active external predecessor can supply the b incoming gains. The sincere comparison is l[T(r-d)/n-K]. Common already-authorized audit costs cancel. If this exceeds (3), the deviation cannot make everyone in the subset better off, even granting compensated members and the specified free internal substitutes. This is an upper-bound test, not a claim that every hidden subcoalition actually has contracts or transfers that attain its upper bound.

Every proper nonempty set has b≥1 and a contiguous block can attain b=1. It suffices to test l=1 and l=n-1 in

    (r-d) - r t^(n-l)m^l > l [(1-t^n)(r-d)+nK].                 (4)

The left side minus the right is a concave function of real l since (m/t)^l is convex. Positivity at the endpoints gives positivity throughout. It also implies positive benefit at increasing b. For a stronger comparison admitting *some* still-authentic controllers inside the deviating coalition but at least one false one, replace every D_l by the upper bound D_1 and grant the subset all the same internal savings. Under these more favorable terms, checking the l=n-1 right side is sufficient for every proper coalition. This grants possible savings that a genuine signer in the subset would in reality have to pay. A fully legitimate substitute that all founders can activate is evaluated on its own all-in price, not labeled a deception. Any covert scheme that passes with a different probability needs that law instead.

For n=3,r=6,d=1,t=0.95,m=0.10,K=0.10 the stronger left side is 5-6(0.95²)(0.10)=4.4585. The largest right side is 2[(1-0.95³)5+0.30]=2.02625. Therefore even this strengthened proper-subset deviation bound leaves strict collective margins. The earlier t=0.5 example certifies the unilateral gate repair; it does not pass this stronger coalitional comparison merely by passing (1). The difference reflects both actual receipt of service and the opportunity to keep a productive subgroup after exit.

**Naturality and exploration ledger.** Positive and negative readback errors and independent deadlines arise for finite equipment checks. An existing configurable evaluator, a readback that distinguishes it from retained objectives, and affordable intersite verification are demanding architectural premises. Where ordinary policy controllers expose the same interface, use their true cost and count their actual behavior. The framing candidate is that founders can choose safeguards suited to the gains at stake. Its structural mechanism selects an evidence rule by its effect on actual incentives and activation. The instance claim “stricter unanimity always helps genuine adoption” is dead at t=0.5. A sibling selects a less restrictive evidence threshold or verifies only the relevant endpoints. A structural successor tests jointly chosen evidence rules against correlations, coalition departures and more informative genuine or conventional local control technologies.
