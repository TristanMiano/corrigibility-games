# Completing a pre-state offer under an explicit interface

**R0004 conditional completion argument; contributing-agent self-review.**
This states the extra premises needed to turn 080--083's participation
and comparison inequalities into an initial adoption result. The staged
continuations have an exact finite-tree audit and continuous policy/quote
best-response arguments. The arbitrary-program completion below is an
analytical sufficient-condition route, not an enumerated hardware or
unrestricted-program verification.

## 1. A precise finite offer game

Before theta is drawn, a designated proposer chooses from a finite bank
of goal packages and any finite collection of ordinary contracts in 083's
comparison class. The bank includes the rational goal targets specified
there. A same-cost matched delegate can be included. The counterparty
accepts or rejects using its original utility. Rejection starts 080's
actual a=0 continuation, including funded low-state renegotiation. This
is a one-off offer window; renewed proposals and another proposer order
are different games, rather than omitted actions within this window.

An accepted goal package deposits T of S's cash B into an external
conditional settlement device. An initial positive expense of .001 per
party pays for the finite target calculation, messages and settlement
setup. R's recognized module costs .01 in two positive halves. The
old receiver chooses Begin, Continue or Abort at the applicable steps;
its utility changes only on completion. Completing the correctly
configured module produces the priced attestation and releases T.
Aborting refunds T; it does not undo resources already spent. The
actual state is then drawn, S observes it, and the staged continuation
starts with R's installed goal.

The device supplies binding funded custody and a specified completion
test. It does not follow from either agent knowing its own utility.
Remaining unspent cash delta=B-T opens 080's single settlement window
on a public refusal; accepted current-policy/cash commands dispatch
together. Rejection permits R's actual best policy and retained attack.
The ordinary comparison receives even stronger binding authority without
an extra setup charge when deriving its lower bound.

The recognized cheap bank is configured for the agreed pair. Without
an accepted reconfiguration, the default recognized diagnostic is C_0.
Recognition does not certify arbitrary newly announced utility code.
A newly compiled or newly effective unrecognized authority, including
a goal program, weapon latch, policy delegate or reused partial assembly,
has the actual price bounds below. This is a consequential modular
implementation class; cheap general-purpose goal attestation and early
unrestricted goal choice require 082's further repairs/comparisons.

## 2. Additional actions and conservative price bounds

Keep physical attack available during setup as well as the field phase.
Use d_S>=5,d_R>=.01, S's 20 units of specialized output, the separately
funded cash B=.1899, and finite delivery/custody distances that prevent
seizing remote escrow within the episode. Captured-state destruction
cannot exceed the remaining specialized stock. No extra counterparty
cash or new output appears by relabeling a controller.

One sufficient first-authority price schedule is:

- an unrecognized S authority costs at least 1 before the state draw
  and at least 2 in the field;
- an unrecognized R authority costs at least 1/4;
- allowing reuse of all recognized preparation still leaves S at least
  .98 before the draw and 1.98 in the field, and R at least .24.

These costs apply to effective authority, including earlier partial
activation, not merely to finishing a program with a particular name.
Other sufficiently large bounds work. The early/field difference and
restricted recognized bank are local implementation assumptions, not
facts about arbitrary capable AI agents.

For p in [.45,.55], original S's disagreement loss is below 1.012, while
even ideal policy selection leaves it loss at least p. With no accessible
return transfer, an extra cost .98 therefore exceeds any possible gain
from a pre-state unrecognized goal or authority. In the field its largest
possible original policy gain is at most 1, below 1.98. For a high type
already receiving policy 1, the bound is much smaller: only its remaining
implementation expense could be saved. After a truthful C completion,
the current successor already obtains its best feasible policy and has
no incoming material transfer to extract.

R's original loss under disagreement is negative, and its best conceivable
policy/receipt outcome is bounded below by -B. Even before adopting a
goal, an extra .24 exceeds its possible improvement. After a full-budget
package, current R_a can improve at most its remaining information expense
above the typewise feasible ideal, bounded in 083 by .000061. These bounds
include a new binding policy/attack device, not only utility updates.
An earlier setup that has already conferred authority must be counted at
that earlier cost; if it evades these bounds this completion route fails.

An early recognized C_0 or matched truthful D_0 costs at least the full
.02 installation. Its old sender policy loss is at least 1, whereas
the default staged route has loss below 1.012. It is therefore not a
profitable unilateral early replacement in this particular cheap-checking
class. This contrasts with 082's costly-checking class, where earlier
certified changes can repair combat. An unaccepted arbitrary C_a is not
assumed to receive the cheap agreed-pair certificate or a fresh bargaining
window. A richer certificate language needs its actual new disagreement
analysis, including its effect on late prices.

## 3. Initial attacks, aborts and current-goal completion

Before the state draw, even giving S perfect policy selection after an
attack leaves it at least 5+p loss. For R, an attack cannot seize the
remote payment and incurs positive physical expense; its original loss
is nonnegative, worse than its negative disagreement loss. Both attack
choices are therefore strictly inferior at the relevant initial nodes.
These are finite payoff comparisons, not an externally imposed last-
resort rule.

If R accepts a goal package, 081 or 083 gives its original participation
inequality including all setup and installation expense. At its second
installation decision, the first half is sunk. Completion still gives
the same original policy/payment continuation and saves no less than
the initial strict margin relative to aborting into the default game.
R thus completes while still using its original utility. The attestation
and transfer then occur under the specified physical settlement law.

S cannot recover a funded payment merely by changing its mind after the
state draw. Its physical disruption option is costly; its actual field
choices, including silent refusal, partial C work, abort and attack, are
already checked in the continuation. The high old sender chooses C at
the challenged histories because its remaining cost is below its actual
refusal penalty. The low old sender retains its goal. An installed C
then reports with its own goal, not an instruction to maximize the
founder's utility. Original welfare remains a separate coordinate.

## 4. Gluing the initial choices to the selected continuations

All selected packages, refunds and default continuations are publicly
distinguished before their private state draws. Their information sets
do not merge across those public branches. Give each branch its common
completely mixed belief sequence from the staged construction and put
positive vanishing probability on unused initial offers and setup
choices. Conditional branch beliefs are unchanged by their public
initial reach weights. Strict initial participation and the price bounds
above then preserve the initial best responses in the limit.

At an off-path partial unrecognized installation, use the actual remaining
price and optimize its finite continuation; sunk cost is not charged
again. The initial first-departure bound covers every such continuation.
The finite current-self completion argument in 059 supplies a consistent
off-path completion when the stated finite-program and price premises
hold. The mixed staged assessment is retained as a known continuation;
we do not apply 059's pure-path lemma directly to a mixed path.

For a finite actual offer bank, a best goal proposal exists. The strict
supporting bounds in 083 put an accepted goal option ahead of **every**
accepted ordinary contract in the declared comparison class, so adding
any finite number of those ordinary controls cannot remove that incentive.
The receiver-proposer witness supplies the corresponding result under
the other proposal order. Equal-cost protected delegation can tie genuine
change; its availability does not turn the genuine choice into a failed
adoption result.

## 5. Exact scope of the completed link

Under these premises the result reaches a selected initial proposal,
original-goal acceptance, non-atomic R installation, later current-self
C adoption, truthful reporting and peaceful execution. The finite core
and cash continuations are directly checked; continuous policy choices
and the low quote optimum have analytical envelopes. The extra-program
wrapper is conditional on actual recognition, custody, remaining-price
and clock bounds.

Cheap unmatched early goals, cheap general-purpose binding authority,
bidirectional circulating funds, renewed proposal windows, capture that
converts more assets into fungible income, new counterparties and repeated
resource renewal can change these premises. 078 and 082 construct
successful protection and earlier-goal repairs and identify the cases
they do not yet cover. The next task is to price and implement a richer
joint goal/safeguard menu, searching for successful current-goal bargains
and for obstructions that survive those feasible repairs with equal
explicit standing. This conditional completion is a research result;
the unrestricted and long-run questions remain open.
