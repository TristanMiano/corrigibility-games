# A founder that protects a minimum stock can still choose a shared goal

**E8, note 167.** Each site gives high priority to keeping enough output for its own mission. Two other sites can help finish that mission's next job. A genuinely shared goal can still be worth adopting when the donors can buy adequate protection for their own minimum stocks. The time at which they can acquire and verify the protection changes who wants to cooperate.

## Measuring a real discontinuity in the original stakes

For a site with own stock q and spendable numeraire v take u(q,v)=q+B·1{q≥L}+v. B measures the value of crossing a minimum useful stock L. A batch too small to run a facility, a minimum delivery obligation or a project requiring a completed series can all produce threshold-like stakes. Smooth steep benefits give the same strict choices away from the threshold. B is finite and positive. Different sites may have different cardinal scales. Positive weights on their actual terminal utilities are genuine common concern.

In the three-site recipe of 166, any site may be the recipient, with equal prior chances. Both others must give up d of their own stock to finish r for the recipient. The baseline w=L+s is the common own stock if all retain their individual production. Each genuine installation consumes k of that own stock. Assume 0<k<s<d+k so that an uncompensated donation after installation crosses the minimum. A job can be conferred upon a claimant after a single pass, honest t and fake m as in 163. Setup, information, trusted timing, control rights and original outside options are priced separately. Ex ante no one initially values another site's stock.

More generally, for any increasing own-stock utility u and stock w, the setup loss in original utility is K=u(w)-u(w-k). Define R_H=u(w-k+r)-u(w-k), R_F=u(w+r)-u(w), D_H=u(w-k)-u(w-k-d). The receipt available for resisting an unconfigured claimant in a cell of likelihood L_z is R_H-L_z R_F. The required input for a true donor is D_H. Substitute these in the complete recipe margin and keep K as the expense. This prices the changed marginal utility after the founder actually spends its stock. Simply using the same percentage of every payee's initial wealth can give the wrong answer. Nor can spending to make oneself needier produce strict adoption from an uninformative signal alone: R_H-R_F≤K by monotonicity at the terminal receiving stock. A fake can receive on the same histories, keeps the expense and avoids all donor losses.

Here R_H=R_F=r, K=k and D_H=d+B. With t=0.9, m=0.1, r=6, d=1, k=0.1, s=0.3 and B=10, even unanimous common concern conserves the donors' stocks rather than complete an unprotected job. A sponsor cannot obtain this project just by raising everyone's concern for everyone else equally. The partner losses are themselves part of the proposed criterion.

## Whose precaution is worth buying?

A reserve unit b=1 suffices to keep the donor above L even after setup and donation. Producing or buying it costs c=1.5 in the separate numeraire, so its net cost, once valued as actual stock, is a=c-b=0.5. A founder doing only its solo work strictly declines it. Its baseline already meets the minimum. The reserve adds stock and consumes explicit resources. It gives no extra production slot. Strength, salvage and agency are as in 166's expiring batch; other long-lived assets are inaccessible to an attacker in this model.

**If a reserve can be bought on the donor's actual call**, donating costs d+min(B,a) relative to retaining the job. The two methods are supplying without protection or buying it and supplying. Buying alone costs a and has no benefit over retention. The honest membership incentive on positive recipient readings is therefore

    (t-m)r - 2t[d+min(B,a)] > 3k.                                   (1)

It includes the fake beneficiary's chance to obtain other people's service without ever helping. The common late objective strictly chooses buying and giving when r>2(d+a) and a<B. Contributions can be sequenced within the window, or jointly released using an actually funded operator. At the displayed numbers, the gross single-founder truth margin is 0.7 and its original expected gain is t[r-2(d+a)]/3-k=0.8; faking would yield 0.2. After the shared goal is active the donors themselves strictly prefer the funded precaution to losing their minimum or missing the productive opportunity. Every real participant can consent under its old objective. A paid local delegate with access to both the reserve order and the donor actuator would offer the same physical protection.

**If the reserve must be bought before the roles are known**, an original with a private inventory choice faces a different game. Paying a each guarantees that every donor can give without crossing L. The original payoff to doing this with the group would be t(r-2d)/3-k-a=0.6. But a genuine signer who privately skips the reserve and still counts as an eligible recipient can get tr/3-k=1.7: when it is the recipient both partners are equipped; when it is a donor its actual common objective prefers to withhold, even as last donor, since d+B>r. It has genuinely changed and can still pass the goal readback. The claimed fully equipped continuation fails as an equilibrium of such **private** early purchases. This deviation challenges the safeguard, even with truthful registration of preferences.

A feasible repair is to require evidence of a separately earmarked, unencumbered reserve before issuing anyone's recipient credential. The supplement must remain available until the job, and stock already promised elsewhere cannot be used twice. A false claimant then either spends a for a possible mr/3 receipt, or gives up receipt altogether; its best return is max(0,mr/3-a). A genuine unequipped founder is likewise denied a claim and cannot improve by buying a useless registration. Since 0.6 exceeds both alternatives, the fully equipped founders strictly participate. A founder can spend another 0.10 on the stock-check service, conditional on a signed agreement, and still prefer it to separate production. If this same group can buy the just-in-time reserve in (1) instead, its higher 0.8 return displaces the more expensive early precaution. Long lead times, availability under demand spikes and actual rights to certify stock decide which comparison is relevant.

## A reserve can fail. When will anyone find out?

Suppose each quick reserve fails independently with probability ε, in which case the site receives zero extra stock after paying c. If failure is revealed **only after donation**, a donor's true expected original cost of buying the reserve and giving is d+a+ε(b+B), because it loses both the promised stock and its threshold benefit when the reserve fails. Its best alternative method of giving costs d+B. At the example's large B the protected method is preferred for the ε considered. Equation (1) becomes strict only for ε<1/11≈0.09091. For ε=0.15 even the group would prefer to keep the two local projects over a blind donation with these reserves.

Instead let the same physical reserve report its success **before the inputs become irrecoverable**. Both requested reserves must be ordered before either result arrives. Success yields stock b even if the joint job is later canceled, and failure still consumes the entire price. The donors choose to release only when both have usable reserves. With B>r a missing reserve makes completion too costly even after another input has sunk. Write p=(1-ε)². Actual completion on the all-genuine path has probability tp. The original welfare of a genuine founder and its false-claim alternative are

    V_H = t[p(r-2d)-2(a+εb)]/3 - k,       V_F = mpr/3.               (2)

The former charges both purchases on every attempted job including cancellations. Strict adoption over bluffing requires p[(t-m)r-2td]>3k+2t(a+εb). This condition implies that the future common goal wants the funded joint order before seeing its results. With publicly sequenced authorization for the two conditional purchase orders, the first and last authorizers both benefit by that completion. Simultaneous unexplained spending can instead coordinate on no order. At ε=0.15 the chance of finished cooperative service is 0.65025, V_H=0.377 and V_F=0.1445. A founder can pay an additional 0.05 for timely testing and still prefer this institution to the zero-gain blind or separate continuation. Other informative probes and spot suppliers compete at their actual expense.

For any finite high B, learning the failure in time to withhold keeps B off the *realized* donor loss in (2). In contrast, acting before the diagnosis leaves a loss proportional to εB. This is a scope claim about finite threshold utilities, protected private stocks and recoverable pre-release control. It does not establish a result for arbitrary lexicographic preferences or a universally reliable long-run safeguard.

**Exploration ledger.** The framing candidate is that negotiated concern and protection of a founder's original mission can be complements. The structural candidate prices safeguards using when they can be bought and whether failure can still be acted upon. The private early-stock plan is dead as an equilibrium at the displayed values, despite the genuine update and a large engineering gain. One sibling makes an earmarked reserve an actual condition of access; another buys only on a donor call. The higher-rung successor asks which combinations of production authority, truthful diagnosis, liquidity and future outside opportunities allow founders to choose protection after they already care about their partners.
