# How much insurance does a genuine common goal actually buy?

**E8, note 160.** In the private three-tier case of 146, the best funded external ordinary contract comes remarkably close to the fully shared goal. It can serve a highest-value site in every state and elicit strictly truthful reports. What it gives up is a small amount of insurance. An exact pair of certificates puts the break-even genuine setup price between **0.0003815 and 0.0003818 output units per founder** when the ordinary device is free. The typical service in this fixture has value between one and three units.

## A feasible ordinary rule and a bound on every external rule

Use n=3, iid true values 1,2,3 with masses (1/2,1/8,3/8), f(y)=y-y²/32 on the feasible interval, κ=1/16 and initial spendable stock 3. Let I_i indicate whether site i is served. If its publicly indistinguishable type a mimics report b, its actual net income is y_i+(a-b)I_i on the b profile. This is exactly the private earnings and funded transfers interface of 146. It covers any equilibrium of an external agreement if a type can imitate the complete observable strategy of another. The public program can see neither the privately realized service value nor an unforgeable report of it.

For any nonnegative multipliers λ_ab, add to aggregate original welfare the λ-weighted sums, over sites of type a, of the interim truthful advantage against mimicking b. At a credible original-goal equilibrium these constraints are nonnegative. The augmented expression therefore provides an upper bound once maximized over all assignments and transfers. Conditional on a report profile t and its possible recipient, the score reduces to

    Σ_i [w_i f(y_i)+I_i (b_i y_i+c_i)],
    w(t)=1+Σ_b λ_tb - Σ_a (p_a/p_t)λ_at,
    b(t)=κ Σ_a (p_a/p_t)λ_at(a-t),
    c(t)=-Σ_a (p_a/p_t)λ_at f(a-t),                             (1)

where w_i=w(t_i), and likewise for b_i,c_i. The identity uses f(y+z)=f(y)+f(z)-κyz. The prior ratio changes a mimicked type-a conditional calculation into a sum on actual type-t profiles. At either displayed multiplier vector all w_i>0. Maximizing this quadratic over even unrestricted real incomes subject only to Σy_i≤x, where x is the selected report's output (zero for idling), gives

    ν=max{0,[Σ_i(1+I_i b_i/w_i)-κx]/Σ_i(1/w_i)},
    y_i=(w_i+I_i b_i-ν)/(κw_i).                                 (2)

This is the concave quadratic optimum by its first-order conditions or completion of squares. Maximizing over each possible winner and idling gives an upper bound covering arbitrary correlated randomization, asymmetric ordinary plans, burned stock and finite deposits. The quadratic extension beyond the reachable interval is used only to relax this maximization. All actually proposed incomes and deviations use the increasing part of the original payoff.

Take λ_21=0.02646 and λ_32=0.01135, all other entries zero. Evaluate (1)–(2) using rational arithmetic. The upper bound on aggregate ordinary welfare is below 2.552649388. To obtain a *feasible* ordinary mechanism, use the maximizing recipient and transfers from the same formula, breaking tied maxima uniformly after reports. In all 27 profiles every selected alternative serves a highest true report, exactly balances funded stock and charges each payer at most its deposit. Directly comparing the six misreports for every site gives minimum interim advantage above 0.0000284. Its aggregate original welfare exceeds 2.552648700. The upper and lower bounds differ by less than 0.00000069; these outward decimals do not assert attainment of the relaxed maximization by an exact optimal multiplier.

The full-insurance benchmark n E f(M/n) is 2.5537312826 to the shown precision. When common-goal members pay k from their own safe stock, the aggregate loss is n[k E f'(M/n)+κk²/2]. Here E M=1347/512 and E f'(M/n)=7743/8192. Inserting the ordinary bounds and solving the positive quadratic root gives the stated price interval. Below its lower endpoint the genuine arrangement strictly benefits every founder relative to *every symmetric* funded external ordinary rule. Above its upper endpoint the displayed ordinary rule strictly beats it for every founder. A matched cheap local controller with access to the true private value can fully insure at the same price as a goal change. Costly external computation, escrow deployment or estimating the type law also belongs in the actual comparison. These price brackets refer to this calibrated example, not to agents or organizations in general.

## What if the founders only know the workload approximately?

The selected ordinary rule prices reports using a nominal workload. A change in the actual iid masses from (0.500,0.125,0.375) to (0.501,0.124,0.375), keeping the rule fixed, makes one high-to-middle deviation profitable. The mistake would be to infer that an external institution inherently needs that much calibration. The founders can choose less finely balanced transfers.

For each deviation let m_ab be its nominal truthful advantage and R_ab the range of its conditional advantage across the two other sites' profiles. Changing one independent marginal by total variation at most δ changes the joint law of the two others by at most 2δ (couple each component). The truthful advantage thus remains strictly positive if 2δ R_ab<m_ab for all deviations. With λ_21=0.03294, λ_32=0.01398, the same literal enumeration constructs a full-routing ordinary rule whose nominal welfare is 2.5520911282 and whose certified radius exceeds 0.02809 per marginal. These richer margins cost about 0.000558 in *aggregate* utility at the nominal prior compared with the first ordinary rule. The winner and others knowingly accept variable receipts to buy more robust private reporting. Which stability of the type law is plausible depends on the project's history, number of observations and rate of change.

By comparison, voluntary equal settlement under the common goal attains the statewise sum maximum in a truthful equilibrium for each of these priors. It still needs correctly specified original utility functions, credible activation and cooperation at the selected equilibrium. Ordinary mechanisms can also buy evidence or use posted prices. The financial insurance problem is a plausible feature of privately valued uses of a scarce job. The precision of the first numerically attractive ordinary rule and the real cost of installing either interface are separate questions.

The [reproducible certificate](../../experiments/r0007_private_insurance.py) checks both rules and the shifted-prior deviation using exact fractions, enumerating all recipient/idle options and all individual deviations. Floating-point optimization was used only to locate candidate rational multipliers; it supplies neither the upper bound nor the feasibility proof.

**Exploration ledger.** Framing: a change that genuinely helps can still have a very small price ceiling. Structural: bound the ordinary institution's foregone insurance while giving it unfettered external agreement design and measuring prior sensitivity. The calibrated and robust certificates are promising at this instance. The reading that accurate ordinary dispatch requires exposing the private true service to a trusted meter is dead here; risk-dependent transfers sustain accurate dispatch with messages alone. Its sibling deliberately increases reporting margins. Its structural successor prices implementation under uncertain beliefs, different risk preferences and actually available local authorities.
