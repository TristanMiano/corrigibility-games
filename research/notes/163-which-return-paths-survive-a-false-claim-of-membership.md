# Which return paths survive a false claim of membership?

**E8, note 163.** A productive return path pays every founder when future favors flow as agreed. If a founder can pretend to change, it can still receive some of those favors while saving both setup and the cost of giving. The relevant return is therefore the service a genuinely configured member can receive *in excess of* what it could obtain by pretending. The founders can deliberately place their inspections at the points where a false member would collect.

## Rights, information, and the timing of the decision

There are finitely many possible jobs e=(i,j), with probabilities λ_e>0. Only one will be called. Keeping gives its donor i an incremental d_e>0, and sending gives its recipient j an incremental r_e>0. Measure changes relative to local use. Everyone initially values its own output, with constant marginal values on this range. Every founder must authorize the same nomination before knowing the job or the evidence. Each can preconfigure the signed conditional update at cost k_i≥0, or sign while retaining its own evaluator and saving that cost. A nomination rejected at signing gives i its actual outside gain o_i≥0. This gain can include an ordinary supplier or enforceable commitment. Distinct outside offers which cannot be used simultaneously can still enter each individual's decision after refusal, provided the specified refusal continuation really delivers them.

After the job is identified the rule can consult evidence z about the configurations and open with probability a_e(z). A successful event actually changes all sincerely configured participants to a single negotiated positive weighted sum of terminal outputs. The update has already been authorized and physically staged. A closed gate leaves the old evaluators active. The evidence can include an agreed low-cost check of just the beneficiary. All possible fake behaviors in this first class reduce to refusing the configuration and keeping instead when called as donor. Signatures, operational readbacks and goal activation are distinct. Unscheduled late edits, useful partial configurations and coalitions are richer deviations. Prepayment to the evidence provider, when required, consumes real work and must be charged. In the linear example this can be a common inspection charge paid by all signers, including a fake, in addition to k_i. Subtract such a charge in the refusal comparison.

The public law of the evidence at job e is P_e if all are genuine. If only j fakes, write Q_je and L_je(z)=Q_je(z)/P_e(z) on positive-probability genuine observations. Set the rule closed at genuine-impossible observations. This only discourages profitable false receipt and never sacrifices genuine service. The finite laws may contain common faults and correlations. A fake donor strictly keeps its job. With honest partners it receives service on incoming jobs that the gate opens. A true donor strictly prefers local use when the gate is closed. Timely contest is available to donors, receivers and bystanders. In this dated version contest spoils service before completion and consumes positive resources, including for a fake. Use the different continuation in 159 when salvage remains valuable.

For gates that actually implement delivery when they open, the sincere founder's gross gain U_i equals its expected incoming r on open jobs less its outgoing d on open jobs. Define two margins after its real setup expense:

    M_i = U_i-k_i - Σ_(e into i) λ_e r_e E_Qie[a_e],
    I_i = U_i-k_i-o_i.                                                (1)

M_i checks a false signature against all other founders configuring sincerely. I_i checks refusal at the outset. If an inspection fee h_i is paid on either kind of signature, replace o_i by o_i+h_i in I_i. A different expense avoided by the fake belongs in M_i too. With sequential signatures and a specified all-sincere configuration equilibrium, strict positivity secures each decision at pivotal histories. Other coordination equilibria may coexist. The gate designer also has to pay acquisition or drafting costs out of its real participation margin.

## A computable test for any finite referral network and evidence experiment

Choose nonnegative numbers p_i,q_i with Σ_i(p_i+q_i)=1, and define

    H(p,q) = Σ_(e=i→j) λ_e E_Pe[
                 ( r_e[p_j(1-L_je)+q_j] - d_e[p_i+q_i] )_+ ]
             - Σ_i (p_i+q_i)k_i - Σ_i q_i o_i.                        (2)

The best attainable minimum of the 2n margins in (1) is min H. In particular the designer can achieve strictly positive truth and participation margins for every site iff this minimum is positive. Moreover, when it is positive **one genuine common goal can execute a feasible improving agreement** even though its choice was constrained by the temptation to fake.

For the value calculation, first allow an open job to be assigned to its recipient by any hypothetical device. The minimum margin equals the minimum convex combination of the 2n margins. Interchange minimization over those weights and maximization over the finite box of conditional opening probabilities by finite-dimensional linear minimax. The coefficient of a given a_e(z) in the weighted objective is its summand before the positive part in (2). Maximizing each independent probability gives exactly that positive part. This upper bound even grants a device the ability to command every desired referral.

For genuine execution, maximize the product of all M_i and I_i over their nonnegative feasible region, equivalently the sum of their logs on the positive region. Feasibility of positive margins guarantees a positive optimum. Write p_i=1/M_i and q_i=1/I_i there, with a common normalization if desired. The directional derivative toward any other feasible gate is nonpositive by optimality, including directions initially remaining in the strictly positive region. Thus the supporting linear objective has a nonnegative coefficient wherever the optimized gate ever opens. Set α_i=p_i+q_i>0. On such a job

    r_e α_j - d_e α_i ≥ r_e p_j L_je(z) ≥ 0.                          (3)

The sincere successor with terminal objective Σα_i U_i^original therefore prefers sending, strictly if the last lower bound is positive or the first inequality is strict. Mutually accepted priority can select sending at equality. This proves constructive sufficiency with the same local sensor and actuator limits. A rare observation on which a false claimant often collects can optimally leave the originals in place even when an unconditional common objective would donate. The conditional activation interface is consequential. Where a founder can profitably rewrite its scheduled decision after reading its role, it needs a credible subscription, a cost of reversal or a different timing calculation.

Genuineness on an open job follows from terminal rankings. The donor previously strictly preferred keeping, and now weakly prefers giving. For a recipient, keeping and wasting the batch yield the same own increment, yet its shared goal strictly favors intact keeping. The same comparison distinguishes the neutral founders' objectives. On closed jobs no terminal evaluator changes. The expected number of actual changes on the all-sincere path is n Σλ_e E_Pe a_e. The price of participation and the probability of actual activation are different optimization targets. The value formula optimizes the **minimum margin**, and its separation test concerns existence of a strictly credible club. It does not assert that its maximizing gate also maximizes the number of changes.

With no outside return and zero expense, the I constraints follow from strict M since false receipt is nonnegative. Discard observations with 1-L_je≤0. Referring in such a cell cannot improve any site's truth margin. Every remaining job-observation gives an ordinary generalized-flow edge with donor cost λ_e P_e(z)d_e and effective gain ratio r_e(1-L_je(z))/d_e. By the source-component proof in 153, all founders can strictly gain and resist faking **iff every source component of this positive-edge graph contains a directed cycle whose effective gains have product greater than one**. Include every founder as a vertex, even one with no eligible edge. Keeping only the largest positive ratio for each ordered pair preserves the zero-cost existence test because each retained observation has positive capacity. For positive prices apply (2), which accounts for how rarely the helpful readings occur. Even with uninformative readbacks a conventional enforceable exchange can be productive under the old goals. Its adoption and authority require separate pricing.

## Checking the site due to receive service

In a homogeneous n-cycle with uniform job probabilities, suppose a site passes its individual inspection with probability t when sincere and m<t when false, with no further evidence about it in the other readings conditional on its own. All founders can preauthorize a recipient-specific test without knowing who the recipient will be. Use no outside return. For any anonymous workload, symmetry of this particular ring and convexity let us average across rotations and use equal weights in the truth-only minimization. Thus the exact greatest unilateral margin before k is

    [(t-m)r-td]_+ / n.                                                (4)

Indeed a positive reading has likelihood ratio m/t and a negative reading (1-m)/(1-t)>1, so only the positive reading can reward sincere receipt enough to pay for a genuine donation. This computes the best truth margin over arbitrary history-independent public gates conditional on the job and these readings. If the fully inspected observations contain additional information about a fake recipient, use their joint likelihood in (2). Positive gross margins are possible at zero setup cost precisely when (1-m/t)r>d. A real membership can be productive (r>d) and still unprofitable to verify if too much incoming service reaches a false member.

More service can be chosen when the truth margin has slack. With probability h allow a negative reading through, and always accept a positive. Write T=t+(1-t)h for all-sincere opening and D=m+(1-m)h for a false claimant's incoming opening. The gross unilateral margin is [T(r-d)-Dr]/n. For n=3, r=6, d=1, t=0.50, m=0.05 and k=0.22 the recipient-only test at h=0 gives 0.73333 before setup. At h=0.45 the honest club activates with probability 0.725, D=0.4775 and its truth margin is about 0.25333 before setup. Each original obtains 0.725×5/3-0.22≈0.98833 before inspection expense. By comparison the role-blind quorum design in 161 with 16% acceptance of a lone positive opens with probability 0.56. Both still require every founder's explicit authorization. Any strict h<0.48125 works here with h≥0. The best role-blind count gate cannot reach the 0.725 opening at this price: its descending-likelihood construction in 161 exhausts its truth budget below 0.569 opening. Because the interfaces differ, the improvement comes from learning the job before choosing whose readback to trust. If the equipment must be inspected before the job, the group could purchase all readbacks early and use only the later recipient's when feasible, at the corresponding additional cost.

**Naturality and frontier.** Scarce scheduled jobs and the ability to check an identified claimant before delivery provide a reason for this timing independent of the proposed outcome. An evidence-bearing configurable terminal evaluator and preauthorized late execution are stronger premises. A common operator may offer cheap binding conventional routing, eliminating the need for change. An ignored false alarm can improve cooperation if the remaining risk is acceptable to the founders who pay for it. The framing candidate concerns the return *reserved for genuine participants*. The structural candidate replaces raw gains on a productive path with the marginal incoming benefits of sincerity and checks its founders' true alternatives. The instance “verify every member to the same standard on every job” is wounded by the higher-service beneficiary-specific rule. Siblings inspect each costly route where a false member could collect or purchase evidence shared across multiple eligible jobs. A higher-rung successor tests the participation and incentive boundaries when a group of members builds an alternative club or when established partners have an ordinary credential that gives almost the same service.
