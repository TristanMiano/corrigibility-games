# Membership before and after the parties know who gets a seat

**E8, note 141.** The framing candidate is that uncertain assignment to future bargaining roles can make parties voluntarily choose a broader goal today. The structural candidate distinguishes willingness to insure against exclusion, the ability to buy one's way in after exclusion, and who will pay to start the agreement. The instance uses the rival slot from 140. Every comparison below is made under the goals current at that decision.

## Role risk creates two different admission thresholds

Write a, b, c for the net certainty-equivalent wealth from independence, membership in a pair, and membership in a trio. These are computed from actual allocations, implementations and costs, as in (140.1). Suppose a<b and a<c<b. An excluded third party can offer equal safe payments t to the two members if acceptance forms a trio. CARA translation means their new certainty equivalents are c+t and the entrant's is c-2t. A strict gain for all three relative to pair and solo exists exactly when

    b-c < t < (c-a)/2,   or equivalently 3c > 2b+a.     (1)

The entrant needs 2t liquid, the payments must be credible before the access rights are surrendered, and the quoted values must include any delivery expenses. With an entrant budget L, also require t<=L/2. At equality strict improvement for all is unavailable. Asymmetric transfers do not lower the sum of the two incumbents' reservations. When both already use a common pair criterion that assigns positive weight to each member's actual original utility, the equal t that strictly improves both also improves the current pair criterion. Compute c using additional broadening expense conditional on that history. A pair's already paid setup is sunk. If the current goal treats past fees or wealth differently, its actual evaluation is required.

Now move the choice before the exogenous determination of who will belong to the privileged pair. Suppose its two seats are drawn uniformly. Acceptance of a broad charter gives the common value c, replacing a lottery over *already uncertain service and role*. Conditional service certainty equivalents can be substituted because E u equals u(CE). Every original strictly prefers the earlier charter exactly when

    c > R = -log[(2 exp(-gamma b)+exp(-gamma a))/3]/gamma.   (2)

With a<b, strict risk aversion gives R < (2b+a)/3. For the open interval R<c<(2b+a)/3, all three support an agreement before learning roles. After the seats are assigned, even unrestricted balanced safe payments cannot buy strict unanimous adhesion at the same net productive values. A sibling repair is a contingent agreement signed while roles are uncertain. A structural successor is to compare the cost and information at each *available date of consent*. Early formation is substantive because the agents choose it under their existing goals.

The width of this interval depends on real exposure. If b-a is small, expansion of (2) gives (2b+a)/3-R = gamma (b-a)^2/9 + O(gamma^2 |b-a|^3). This makes small bargaining gains in a small-risk fixture economically fragile. Count the real cost of early preparation against the width. For unequal role probabilities pi_i, an original strictly accepts when u(c)>pi_i u(b)+(1-pi_i)u(a). Someone who already knows it will occupy a seat has the full b reservation. To realize ex ante role uncertainty, the negotiation must genuinely precede assignment, e.g. before selecting a dispatch lead for an unassigned project. An analyst's decision to hide an already-known entitlement does not supply it.

**Physical fixture.** In 140 take iid high probability 1/2, gamma=1, l=1/4, h=3/4 and funded positive k_2=log(101/100). Relative to common w, exp(-a)=5/6, exp(-b)=(101/100)(19/24); exp(-c)=exp(k_3)37/48. Let exp(-c) lie strictly between the weighted geometric and arithmetic means of exp(-a) and exp(-b) with weights 1/3,2/3. This selects a positive interval of real k_3 and gives exactly the early-only region above. The fixture identifies the size and origin of the effect. Equal odds are helpful when positions have similar access. A known lead or a predictable successor needs the heterogeneous role-probability test.

## An actual proposer has to incur the drafting expense

An unassigned project whose productive timing is known can offer a public design window before the role draw. A party may draft the broad charter and bear expense f>0 in safe private work. A valid design can then be accepted by everyone at costs already in c. If nobody drafts, the role lottery gives R. Assume c>R, all can fund f, and once a proposal exists each signer has a current-goal reason to accept. The drafter has to compare c-f with R. After incurring f, acceptance is evaluated with that expense sunk. The mechanism can be a genuine reciprocal charter or an ordinary device with the same capabilities; take the actual best mechanism when setting c.

If c-f>R there are pure volunteer equilibria with one drafter. No-drafter is then vulnerable to an individual deviation and a second drafter can save f by relying on the first. If c-f<R, none drafts in equilibrium: drafting gives less than even the worst no-draft outcome. These statements assume duplicated independent proposals pay real costs and one valid draft suffices. In the interior regime the symmetric mixed equilibrium has probability s that each of n=3 potential drafters waits, with

    s^(n-1) = [exp(gamma f)-1]/[exp(gamma(c-R))-1].        (3)

To verify, equalize utility from drafting and utility from waiting when another draft arrives with probability 1-s^(n-1). Broad implementation occurs with probability 1-s^n under this specified selection. A designated last drafter removes costly duplication when waiting is safe. If delaying exposes the project to a probability d of role assignment before that draft, an earlier agent strictly prefers drafting now to a certain later costless charter conditional on continued availability when

    d > [exp(gamma f)-1]/[exp(gamma(c-R))-1].

This is the current chooser's comparison with an explicitly supplied later sponsor. Role rotation can widen support and public-good drafting can delay it. Both mechanisms depend on actual timing and alternatives.

**Natural or constructed conditions.** The need to fund an initial design and the risk of drawing a privileged or excluded role arise in associations that organize capacity before requests arrive. Equal roles and CARA make costs measurable. Parties can choose an earlier review at real expense when (2) covers it and someone meets the drafting inequality. If scheduling or corporate authority reserves all such decisions to a known incumbent, that construction is unavailable. Substantial costly escrow, delegating ordinary capacity rights or repeated exchange can alter c, b and a. Full common-goal evaluation and finite physical combat from 140 apply when using its short-lived slot. The thresholds are also useful for other payoffs once they have an independently justified continuation.

**Exploration record.** The instance reading “a unanimously attractive early charter can always compensate a privileged pair after the draw” fails on the strict interval in (2). The time at which parties can bind their future selves is consequential. A sibling asks whether a funded pre-draw subscription implements the early charter. The structural successor asks for the insurable component of the enlarging coalition's opportunity across actual information dates. A separate candidate lets the outsider pay for real new productive capacity instead of moving existing wealth. Whether anyone buys it depends on its effect on both productive payoffs and outside options.
