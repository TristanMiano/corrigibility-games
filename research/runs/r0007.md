## LESSONS FROM FAILURES

Pair histories can miss a third site's distinctive demand. Measure demand conditional on the other two being quiet, then ask which facts identify a whole coalition's return. A continuous full-insurance rule can reward understatement, while both a binary funded lottery and some three-tier agreements give ordinary truthful insurance. A scope-specific failure leads to a search for other credible uses of information. Observed peace can also come from uncertainty about later conflict. Include that continuation in any inferred adoption count.
