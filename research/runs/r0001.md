- **Modification and matched delegation can each implement beneficial commitment:** R113/R124 compare revocable policies, installed goals and matched binding execution. - **Broader goals implement more agreements, with explicit accuracy and information boundaries:** R122/R132 recover deterministic positive-surplus implementation beyond R121's proportional-goal family. R152/R174 give sharp concave/nonconcave regret allowances; R175 locates regularity and physical-state sensitivity. R164–R167 give a necessary and sufficient finite stochastic implementation criterion, a successful disjoint-support region, overlap obstructions, and conditional checks after public signals.
- **Information and preferences yield conditional peace and combat regions:** R130–R131 characterize when inspection and credible continuation responses deter deception. R145–R148 distinguish pooled participation, proposer screening and public information, including greater surplus reducing combat in the specified offer game. R158–R160 compare unchanged-goal splits and modification against relative preferences and finite combat-event bonuses using the full stated peaceful menu.
- **Valuable renewed opportunities and specified matching conditions favor change; other regions sustain retention:** R149–R151 yield eventual voluntary first change when sufficiently valuable offers recur, and retention when all gains fall below the change penalty. R140–R142/R168–R169/R177 give frozen-type persistence under random matching, elimination when same-type matching reaches 50/123, and balanced-goal fixation from 2/3 in the zero-innovation process; innovation and local stability have separately stated scopes. R170–R172 permit repeated current-self changes with fresh evaluator memory. Individual utility, voluntary choice and reproductive prevalence remain distinct.

These conditional results establish voluntary-change witnesses, successful repairs and characterized boundaries. Integration into a broader equilibrium explanation, representative prevalence and the additional properties of human-oriented AI corrigibility remain open. Those stronger questions neither erase the achieved constructive results nor acquire answers from the local failure cases. Much of the mathematical machinery is standard; no literature priority is claimed.
