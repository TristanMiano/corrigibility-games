## Failed approaches and audit corrections retained

Raw utility entries are not outcome probabilities. Weighted linear goals do not uniquely implement a proportional split, and arbitrary vNM objectives cannot uniquely select an unconstrained interior lottery. Recursive concave aggregation can depend on merger order; positive affine calibration and lottery preferences matter.

A beneficial endpoint does not establish initial adoption, safe staging, coalition stability or population prevalence. Unsecured prepayment failed its handover check. Detection failed to deter when cheap repair made rejection noncredible. A finite freeze could protect one proposal menu while a stronger goal-preserving transfer bypassed it. Exact optimization failed after a physical budget change. Fixed-state peace failed to prevent earlier strength-investment preemption.

The serial scheduling error was corrected rather than hidden. The private-strength audit also clarifies that the lower pooling threshold does not apply to publicly revealing every type before B's retained attack decision. Restoring a goal's code does not automatically restore its old first-change memory.
