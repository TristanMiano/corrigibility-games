## What the evidence explains

The constructive question is when founders with different existing goals willingly purchase a genuine change because it improves what their partner will do for them. E9 connects five decisions. The owners choose an offer and how to measure it. The original supplier chooses whether to try. A physical process implements or fails to implement the new ordering. Its successor chooses what to do under its actual goal and information. On each observation the other founder may trust, renegotiate, return or contest. The evidence and the distribution of actual goals must agree with those choices. [Commissioning equilibrium](../notes/207-who-can-walk-away-from-a-doubtful-installation.md) · [Finite agreements with separate histories of effort and success](../notes/262-which-agreements-can-use-evidence-of-both-effort-and-goal.md)

- **Actual change, deception and skill.** On the equal-strength factory's working equilibrium, 64% genuinely change, 16% try and fail, and 20% knowingly claim an omitted procedure. A positive reading warrants 16/17 confidence on this equipment. In a different small-to-large order, funding lessons after failures makes some previously attractive imitations unprofitable. It increases genuine installation to 62%, even though many failed pilots have the correct goal. [Commissioning](../notes/206-when-a-truthful-attempt-can-fail.md) · [Training](../notes/245-when-helping-after-a-failed-pilot-makes-success-more-informative.md)
- **Consent at less than certainty.** In the insured-gift example both founders willingly try with uncertain quantities and just 9/16 chance of two actual changes. With known marginal reliability .75 but unknown dependence, a single suitably funded agreement secures consent over the stated laws above a joint-success lower bound of about .55747. Both signers can buy sufficient common scheduling time to guarantee .60. With several possible technologies and different forecasts they also need the *same* price to work for both. [Risky gifts](../notes/215-who-signs-when-even-a-correct-goal-has-an-uncertain-yield.md) · [Joint reliability](../notes/270-how-certain-must-the-partners-be-about-joint-success.md) · [Price under ambiguity](../notes/280-when-the-same-bargain-works-across-disagreeing-forecasts.md)
- **What can be verified.** Finite stochastic evaluators with common grounded histories admit an exact comparison over lotteries even in independently chosen utility units. A counterexample needs at most three short histories. The certificate concerns the live evaluator during its period of authority. A prior-state witness and a comparison with what would happen without this offer answer additional historical and causal questions. A bound on the cost of the wrong ordering can itself justify entrusting uncertain output. [Exact preferences](../notes/286-checking-an-agreed-ordering-without-a-shared-utility-scale.md) · [Partial agreement](../notes/287-how-much-a-buyer-can-lose-when-a-goal-is-only-approximately-right.md) · [Causal attribution](../notes/244-which-changes-did-the-bargain-cause.md)
- **Who can turn uncertainty into a fight.** With an 80% chance of winning, a buyer contests 26% of original offers even though all suppliers attempted the change. When a failure reveals vulnerability, a custodian can keep refusals peaceful and serve all the genuinely changed at about 92% confidence on accepted jobs. At high spoilage the best available disclosure can instead produce 6.65% contested encounters. The original owners would pay up to about .09971 per offer to eliminate the remaining disputes. [Power and installation ability](../notes/250-how-installation-reliability-and-physical-power-interact.md) · [Optimal disclosure and a priced settlement](../notes/285-when-founders-will-pay-to-remove-the-last-disputes.md)
- **Which forms of power travel to other environments.** In a shared water project, unequal rights to make the decisive appointment can cause the weaker district to veto bilateral changes. The stronger district can buy a better installation process or agree to be the sole party that changes. Among wrong choices on the original rotation, 6/11 came from an actually impartial representative with an incorrect forecast. In repeated trade, fresh uncertain byproducts can sustain continued trust in the same recruit under a checked patience boundary. Paid reversible arrangements and loss of separate agency in a merger give different comparisons again. [Public authority](../notes/284-who-trusts-another-district-to-choose-under-an-uncertain-forecast.md) · [Persistent partners](../notes/283-when-an-unchanged-partner-can-also-have-a-lucky-delivery.md) · [Two vetoes on a merger](../notes/260-when-founders-will-merge-under-an-uncertain-joint-goal.md)

The original and the changed controller evaluate different decisions. The buyer who can open the line, the supplier who can withhold effort, a laboratory with a commercial interest and a dispatcher who can reassign equipment have distinct powers. Calibration, enrollment compensation, credible custody, paid insurance and a conditional appointment can be individually or jointly worthwhile. The work tests admission without an edit, existing compatible partners, performance liens, ordinary actuators, delayed disclosure, transfers and rework on the same inputs. In some cases a priced change wins both vetoes, in others a delegate can implement the same outcome, and in still others an ordinary device strictly improves both founders' positions. When a lien can guarantee the most fragile work, the partners can pay to move the most capable installers to ordinary work instead. ### Lessons from failures

- A successful pilot can reveal only skill when future work rewards imitation. The sibling offers a lesson to failed pilots; the higher-level successor jointly chooses education, evidence and admission.
- A charge for truthful evidence can invite combat by exposing the rejected site's weakness. The sibling buys a funded return instrument; the higher-level successor allows competing custodians and sale of the individual evidence.
- Requiring a canonical build excludes genuinely equivalent preferences. The sibling checks a live preference ordering in independent numerical units; the broader candidate prices the material cost of plausible disagreements on the attainable decisions.
- A certain positive certificate can leave many actual installations idle. The sibling buys truthful but less revealing recommendations; the structural successor optimizes the entire consent, service and conflict frontier with endogenous access to raw readings.

### Candidate decision questions (for consolidation)

1. **F1.** Can repeated provider entry sustain a strictly positive time share of voluntary change with a feasible ordinary alternative? Specify entry costs and incumbent rights, solve each contract and replacement decision, then compute occupancy separately from inheritance.
2. **F2.** Does any peaceful revenue-sharing custodial agreement survive a second seller's profitable individual disclosure? Compare the outsider's deviation with every funded acquisition or settlement the original founders can actually arrange.
3. **F3/F5.** Can finite guaranteed accuracy survive an endogenous extension of the output menu and a chance of controller replacement? Specify both transition laws, exhibit a protected continuation or a disagreeing pair of reachable lotteries, and test each founder's willingness to pay.
4. **F4/F7.** Can a jointly priced diagnostic distinguish low skill from goal disagreement well enough to change what the signers do? Check what each possible trial reveals under every incentive-compatible choice of trials, then compare the same extra information offered to an ordinary controller.
