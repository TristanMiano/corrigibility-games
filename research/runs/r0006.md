## Conditional explanation and agreement design

**Shared knowledge plus credible reciprocity and affordable execution favors
consensual genuine change.** Negotiated asymmetric weights broaden the region;
paid standardization can enlarge it. Binding final capacity, expensive checking
or a better ordinary route can instead select retention. An equally capable
ordinary controller at equal cost can tie the genuine route. Revocable effort
promises, binding controllers and permanent ordinary delegation are explicitly
different capabilities; none is silently equated with self-knowledge.

**Useful capture plus sufficient strength and expensive accepted transfers
favors combat in the declared offer game.** Cheap execution, chosen protection
or earlier negotiation can reverse that choice. Increasing overlap can jointly
save setup and increase the prize of capture; the derived net comparison includes
both. Finite damage, recovery work and delay remain physical, and combat remains
available at the relevant nonterminal histories. A failed sufficient peace
bound is never itself treated as proof of fighting.

**A genuine utility change can save future implementation expense when productive
opportunities renew.** The actual alternative includes waiting, later adoption
and ordinary temporary or durable controllers. With a fixed useful research
scope, original maximizers eventually finish the research themselves; paid
creation of new useful scope is a separate constructive intervention.

The original agreement spaces are progressively enlarged by weighted goals,
transfers, conditional installation, paid audits, current-goal renegotiation,
partner access and safeguards. Worked failures are classified as failures of
those particular protocols; several have explicit successful repairs. Stronger
claims remain open where richer private information, weapons, funding, offer
rights or future innovation change the feasible set. The defensive cap theorem
covers its fixed-stock mixed-offer class, not all possible deterrence designs.
Unilateral defensive updates are counted separately from consensual changes.

The [findings report](../reports/r0006-findings.md) gives readable mechanisms,
examples and thresholds, from no productive connection to nearly all shared
upstream steps. Its intervention discussion identifies actors, actual actions,
funded expenses, timing and responses. Simple physical mechanisms and open
parameter regions motivate these families; recognition, conditional goals and
calibrated safeguards are more demanding designed conditions. No distribution
or population-frequency claim follows from either type of example.
