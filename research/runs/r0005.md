## Mathematics and conditional explanation

Claims **R501–R522** and assumptions **A102–A120** record exact scope and proof
locations. E6 connects the following results:

1. **Investment can pay for both originals' genuine changes.** In the
   owner-proposer project, ordinary peaceful bargaining induces effort
   `theta a/c`. Negotiated goals concerning proportional net gains support
   efficient effort `a/c`. Relative to that active ordinary continuation,
   a strictly acceptable share interval exists when combined setup is below
   `(a²/c)(1-theta)²/2`, with actual alternatives and funding also satisfied.
   The exact example raises original returns from `(0.66,0.06)` to
   `(0.9,0.9)` after setup. Both selected paths are peaceful. See
   [110](../notes/110-investment-surplus-and-voluntary-productive-partners.md),
   R513–R517.
2. **A balanced productive maximum supports continued accommodation.** The
   weighted-minimum goals obey `G_i<=gamma_i sum W`. An attainable efficient
   proportional allocation reaches every current goal's maximum. This bounds
   actual outcomes from later utility changes, attacks and demands without
   assigning value to frozen code. Gross versus net references, risk sharing
   and expansion of the physical frontier matter. An incumbent gross-receipt
   goal can approve a further genuine net-gain revision. See
   [107](../notes/107-reusable-goals-across-several-partners.md),
   [108](../notes/108-productive-capital-and-dynamic-balanced-goals.md), 110;
   R511/R514.
3. **Renewal and partners matter through relative implemented value.**
   Stationary, observed-arrival and Markov comparisons include waiting and
   ordinary alternatives. Route-dependent future access changes the stopping
   equation. A funded access purchase can be chosen; better ordinary access
   can instead displace an update while improving welfare. Assignment and
   noncrowding-network certificates cover different capacity classes. See
   [099](../notes/099-renewal-adoption-and-voluntary-assignment.md),
   [103](../notes/103-renewable-markets-and-the-option-to-wait.md), 107 and
   [111](../notes/111-cooperation-that-changes-future-opportunities.md);
   R507–R509.
4. **Actual financing and joint capability changes alter consent.** A priced
   owner advance raises the displayed project's net value from 1.5 to 1.725;
   both installed parties prefer it. At fixed input wealth, increasing output
   efficiency eventually makes both routes hit the same funding constraint.
   In the capital model, patience improves both peaceful production and
   postwar rebuilding; the fixed-share consent effect has sign `gamma_i-p_i`.
   Renegotiating shares repairs the displayed lost consent. See 108/110;
   R512/R515.
5. **Concern's scope and capturable value determine coalition incentives.**
   The symmetric war-deviation core exists exactly when
   `M>=max_k(n/k)(p_k L-c_k)` under the declared contest and transfer class.
   Narrow group concern can favor capture. Broader concern can support peace,
   but voluntary compensation of a defeated attacker creates a middle combat
   region in the worked example. A guard repairs direct attack; a cheap later
   threat extracts tribute from that guard. The stronger common-maximum goal
   rejects that improvement. See
   [109](../notes/109-partner-coalitions-and-the-scope-of-concern.md), R518–R520.
