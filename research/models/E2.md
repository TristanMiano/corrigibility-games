# E2.1 — strength, compromise, merger, and deception

**Start with [When agents favor combat or compromise](../reports/r0001-findings.md)** for the first round's headlines, worked examples and parameter guide. Project seekers prefer credible divisions that beat their combat returns. Revocable sole control favors attack by the excluded agent; private-strength bargaining favors risky offers when the proposer's retained share outweighs its expected combat loss. Goal changes support cooperation when their benefits under the incumbent goal exceed their costs, while a successor's wartime generosity raises the opponent's attack payoff. The report explains these conditions, the role of goal-preservation preferences, and the scope still to be explored. This document supplies the technical model family. The project remains in CG-R001; CG-03 is blocked.

**First-recurrence model family; provisional, 2026-10-30.** This improves the [seed essay](https://thothhermes.substack.com/p/the-corrigibility-folk-theorem) within [contract C1](../MODEL.md), preserving strength-relative control, strength-parameterized probabilistic combat, and split/merge/deception mechanisms. It is not a replacement for C1 or a permanent recurrence default. The completed [r0001 record](../runs/r0001.md) links the proofs, counterexamples and accounting. The finite-horizon witness **E2-U** is specified in [note 010, section 14](../notes/010-essay-formalization.md#14-persistent-attack-can-make-unilateral-change-attractive-without-convergence); sections 15–17 classify finite freezing and installation reliability. Further modules deliberately vary its assumptions. This family is a specification and comparison framework, not one extensive game in which every module has simultaneously been solved.

## 1. Primitives that remain open

At history h the live controllers are I(h), with effective strengths a_i(h)>0, current preference classes [u_i^h], physical state z(h), and implementation state m(h). An inactive identity can have strength zero and no decisions while its original welfare remains meaningful. Physical state contains usable/dispersed material O,D; delivered color quantities x_i; available work B; production/infrastructure capacity k; and remaining time T. Add transformed feedstocks, spatial structure, temperature, ownership or repair state when the question requires them. The local recovery module uses divisible quantities; that is not a family invariant.

Complete outcomes include physical production, control/agency, utility changes, and any process events valued by the chosen preferences. Expected utility is over **actual histories**, not reported goals or new utilities' numerical approval of themselves. Positive affine recodings are the same expected-utility preference class. Founders evaluate installing a successor using their pre-installation criterion; every later decision uses its then-current criterion. Founder welfare is reported separately.

Each concrete game supplies: finite horizon or other well-posed evaluation; all action menus; who moves and collision priority; physical/agency/utility kernels; observation and belief kernels; installation steps, failure and rollback; ordinary peaceful controls; and a decision-self/sequential solution concept. Public labels, correct semantic verification, atomic transactions and irreversibility are **separate parameters**. For claims below, common contest probabilities and the explicitly named information assumptions apply locally.

Preference equivalence is defined on a common declared outcome/lottery domain, not inferred from equal behavior in one situation. A finite template library can be checked extensionally; a general semantic verifier is not assumed. Code restoration can initialize a fresh evaluator memory, especially for a first-future-change penalty. [Note 029](../notes/029-evaluator-memory-and-reinitialization.md) specifies that distinction and a finite-memory existence subclass. [Note 018](../notes/018-current-self-games-and-robustness.md) supplies finite/discounted fixed-criterion subclasses; neither turns equilibrium existence into peace or goal convergence.

## 2. Strength must act through a declared mechanism

For a complete joint strategy profile sigma, a stochastic-control reading of the essay is

$$P_a^\sigma=\sum_{i\in I(h)}\frac{a_i}{\sum_j a_j}K_i^\sigma,$$

where K_i^sigma is the continuation law conditional on i being selected, including other agents' attacks and responses. Utilities guide policies; they are not probability vectors. Independently controllable conditional laws K_i^sigma=mu_i(sigma_i) are a stronger special case. The device's transfer authority, duration, costs and observation must be specified; selection is not an automatic right to enforce any lottery. A territorial interpretation instead assigns a_i/sum a_j of divisible productive resources and yields a composite world. These mechanisms can have equal ideal linear means but different equilibria when attacks remain possible, as [note 030](../notes/030-control-rights-and-strength-mixtures.md) shows. Ownership, modularity and combat funding belong in the physical authority map.

Combat between capable A and B has the local contest rule

$$q_A(h)=\frac{a_A(h)}{a_A(h)+a_B(h)}.$$

The same strength coordinate therefore governs ordinary control and combat probability in the baseline module. Generalized weights f(a), changing power, asymmetric initiative and private estimates remain available variants. With additive physical strength, requiring merger to preserve influence against every outsider forces f(a)=constant times a (note 010, section 2); nonlinearity can instead represent genuine physical synergy, which must be stated. No contest draw is known in advance merely because its law is known.

Conditional on victory, specify loss of agency, capture/destruction of capacity, current survivor objectives and resulting production. The losing founder's utility is **not** automatically zero. It is zero in the linear opposing-color benchmark only because the selfish winner produces none of the losing color. A shared-goal survivor can still produce that color. When all relevant agents are incapable, define recovery or termination explicitly rather than dividing by zero in the contest formula.

## 3. Four principal regimes, with a peaceful fallback

| Regime | Mechanism to declare | What is not assumed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| War | An available attack triggers the strength contest and outcome-conditioned physical/agency transitions | Infinite cost, automatic extinction of original welfare, or certain victory |
| Agree to split | Agents retain separate identities; a specified production/transfer policy gives an allocation or lottery | Agreement alone enforces that policy or deletes later attacks |
| Merge | Consensual implementation produces a joint controller/objective and explicit combined infrastructure/agency state | Raw weighted utility sums yield proportional outputs; installation is atomic; fusion is equivalent to two agents sharing goals |
| Deception | A declaration/implementation differs from the promised physical policy; observation depends on verification and the actual state | Reports determine utility; detection occurs surely; punishment is credible |

These are principal **mechanisms and history features**, not mutually exclusive exhaustive terminal outcomes: deception can be followed by combat, a rejected merger can lead to ordinary trade, and an agreement can be attacked during recovery. Include Keep/Wait, ordinary work or the specified noncombat control mechanism, further offers and rejection. Rejecting modification does not imply war.

At every relevant nonterminal checkpoint, each capable agent has Attack. The concrete protocol declares interruptions during communication, verification, updating, production and postagreement work, including what costs are already sunk. A finite processing slot may be atomic only at its declared time resolution. An idealized exclusive-control production episode must explicitly remove the other's capacity to intervene, as in the section 13 witness; it proves nothing about stability between two still-capable agents during that episode. After actual physical fusion there are no longer two independent internal attackers, but external attacks and any remaining independent controllers persist.

## 4. Physical transition module

A route j may spend e_j work and ell_j time, disperse d_j usable material, and damage/capture capacity. A simple admissible transition is

$$O'=O-d_j,\ D'=D+d_j,\ B'=B-e_j,\ T'=T-\ell_j,\ k'=k_j,$$

with 0<=d_j<=O, 0<=e_j<=B, 0<=ell_j<=T, k_j>=0. Combat and peace each require their own parameters, with outcome-dependent parameters allowed. Costs of messages, verification, computation, integration and rollback belong in these transitions. There is no assumed ordering between peaceful and combat costs. Routes that cannot pay their cost are infeasible; they do not create negative reserves. Strength-to-capacity conversion is declared separately, e.g. k_P=eta(a+b), k_Wi=eta lambda_i(a_i+rho_i a_j), 0<=lambda_i,rho_i<=1.

For fixed control and a literal serial recovery/production line, let a=max(tau,1/k') when k'>0. If k'=0, production is incapable and its output is zero. Choose recovered r_D and total delivered y satisfying

$$0\le r_D\le D',\quad 0\le y\le O'+r_D,$$
$$cy+wr_D\le B',\quad ay+rr_D\le T',$$

where c,tau>0 and w,r>=0. Material after production is O'+r_D-y usable, D'-r_D dispersed, plus y delivered; their sum remains O+D. Work is a finite store, not total energy; the abstraction does not identify informational entropy, thermodynamic entropy, utility or useful work. Infrastructure loss may need additional material coordinates if their mass is inside the chosen boundary.

The exact attainable serial frontier is

$$Y(z')=\min\{O'+D',\ B'/c,\ (B'+wO')/(c+w),\ T'/a,\ (T'+rO')/(a+r)\}.$$

This corrects E2.0's separate capacity bound, which could overestimate serial output when infrastructure slowed production. [Note 024](../notes/024-recovery-scheduling-and-infrastructure.md) preserves the aggregate LP, proves this correction, and derives alternatives for parallel processing with startup delay and two damaged material classes. E2-U's k=tau=1 numerical witnesses are unaffected. The frontier still conditions on fixed control; use it in a game only after establishing implementation and interruption behavior. Let P_S,P_M,W_A,W_B be actual frontiers after split, merger and the two war results. Note 010 derives how those route outputs, asymmetric winners and peaceful transaction costs affect the bargaining range.

## 5. Successor objectives and incumbent participation

For deterministic divisible output, a family of joint objectives is

$$G_a(x)=\sum_i a_i\phi(x_i/a_i),$$

with phi continuous, strictly increasing and strictly concave. Its unique optimum on sum x_i<=Y is x_i=a_iY/sum a_j. The weights are fixed at installation in this construction; later power/agency changes do not silently rewrite them or delete a defeated founder's color. A state-contingent weighting rule is a different declared objective. This is one constructive objective menu, not a restriction on future utility classes. The seed essay itself flags the failure of a plain weighted linear objective in footnote 4; the construction supplies one repair. Literal addition of arbitrary utility representations is not generally meaningful without normalization/interpersonal conventions.

With linear original color goals and selfish postwar survivors, the disagreement vector is (sW_A,(1-s)W_B). Flexible split peace exists strictly above that vector iff P_S>sW_A+(1-s)W_B. A fixed strength-proportional split requires P_S>max(W_A,W_B), a stronger condition. An analogous merger must pay its own physical costs and any actual pre-change freezing/identity penalties. Objective G_a approving the result does not establish founders' adoption. For general goals, a representation-invariant bargaining rule over expected-utility gains is possible, but it need not be implementable by a successor's expected-utility objective (note 010, sections 11–12).

A single successor choosing from all lotteries cannot uniquely prefer a nondegenerate target lottery. A deterministic mixed-color world, a constrained physical action kernel, and an enforceable randomization program are three different repairs. State which one is used.

## 6. Deception and verification

Let x be actual output/implementation, m its declaration, v verification effort, and o drawn from an observation kernel O(o|x,m,v). Actual x and history determine utility. Distinguish code equality, observed behavior, semantic verification and binding future execution. Public current utility is an optional information assumption, not a guarantee of honest implementation.

Define honest, undetected and detected continuation values H_i,G_i,L_i by solving or otherwise specifying the continuation after each observation. With detection probability d, deception yields (1-d)G_i+dL_i. Note 010 gives the exact threshold, a strong-agent cheating example, a noncredible-punishment counterexample, and a costly-inspection equilibrium. Those comparisons assume the branch values are implementable; detected deception does not automatically trigger war. A camouflaged object that really satisfies both original goals expands the feasible set rather than merely deceiving an evaluator.

## 7. Results, comparisons, and remaining parameters

The [proof note](../notes/010-essay-formalization.md) separates bargain existence, incumbent adoption, checkpoint attack deterrence, equilibrium selection and conditional policy replication. None establishes generic preference convergence, coalition stability or population prevalence. The [claim ledger](../CLAIMS.md) records exact scopes and review status.

Keep four controls visible: unchanged utilities with flexible peaceful policies; enforceable fixed-utility commitments/delegation; voluntary modification/fusion; and combat. Match information, physical costs, failure, available attacks and execution capability. A fixed-utility delegate that reproduces every contingent action, kernel and opponent response reproduces founder welfare; intrinsic modification/identity preferences can break that equivalence. Granting a stronger delegate is a labeled comparison, not a proof that revocable ordinary policies suffice.

## 9. Composition rules and remaining gaps

Modules may be combined only after their shared physical histories, criteria, actions and information agree. In particular:

- The inspection values in 013 come from 012's homothetic partial controller. Replacing it by 014/028/031's goals changes survivor output and requires recomputing honest, deceptive and rejected continuations.
- The population map in 019 uses the exact selected E2-U encounter table and one-batch template inheritance. Adding new goals, positive action errors, private types or another selection changes the birth kernel before any population theorem applies. Section 9 gives only a local result for explicit nearby continuously differentiable perturbations.
- The first-change stopping problem in 022 absorbs the already-valued successor continuation. The recurrent fresh-evaluator model in 029 has different memory and decision authority; neither is a theorem about an unrenewed finite universe.
- The private-information game in 021 does not inherit public-state equilibrium existence from 018/029. Public report timing also changes its later attack constraints.
- Serial and parallel recovery in 024 are alternative technologies. G and the quantity-equivalent Q in 025 have different lottery preferences. A schedule-Lipschitz bound in 028 is different from a utility-Lipschitz bound in 031.
- The staged certificate in 027 is the route for replacing an atomic branch: match every relevant continuation evaluation and check every newly exposed deviation. Matching an intended final output alone is insufficient.

Still open are an integrated repeated bargaining model deriving verification, opportunity arrival and endogenous strength together; implementation costs tied to a physical computing/verification mechanism; arbitrary new preference types and coalition formation; and continuous-action refinements with realistic execution errors. Finite menus, perfect public semantics, selected fallbacks and protected terminal delivery remain labeled local assumptions. Future recurrences may replace any of these choices or leave the seed model entirely. C1's open choices remain intact; its illustrative recovery LP is clarified as an aggregate relaxation rather than an automatically attainable serial schedule.
