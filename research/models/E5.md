# E5 — funded accommodation, observable progress and chosen protection

**R0004 consolidated model family.** Read the [findings](../reports/r0004-findings.md)
for the explanation. E5 extends E4's diagnostic mechanism through observable
progress, actual cash bargaining, a stronger ordinary-control frontier and
conditional initial adoption. It also derives outcome-goal and protection
conditions for combat and continued accommodation. The flexible contract
[C1](../MODEL.md) remains unchanged. These are local instances, not new
mandatory assumptions for future recurrences. Review is contributing AI
agent self-check; CG-03 remains blocked.

## 1. Utilities, state and physical authority

Nature draws theta in {0,1}, with P(high)=p. Sender S knows it; receiver R
controls policy y in [0,1]. Original utilities are
X_S-(y-theta-b)^2 and X_R-(y-theta)^2, b>=1. A negotiated receiver target a
gives its successor X_R-(y-theta-a)^2. S can later install the genuine goal
C_a=X_S-(y-theta-a)^2. Each installation is evaluated by the incumbent;
the installed successor then acts on its own criterion. Original welfare
is archived separately. [073](../notes/073-observable-progress-and-staged-screening.md)
exhibits a common high-state lottery whose ranking reverses.

X_i measures useful own-project output. The physical instance separates
usable, spent and dispersed components, work, fuel energy, heat, recovery
infrastructure and delivery time. Repair is optional and consumes real
work/material/time. The finite ten-tick realization in
[076](../notes/076-causal-frames-and-recovery-costs.md) gives initial effective
contest losses 5 and .01 from dispersion 5.009 and .019. Later recovery can
be harder. Neither energy nor matter is identified with utility.

Combat remains available at live histories. Public, state-independent
strengths give sender victory probability s. A winner learns the state and
controls this project's actuator using its actual criterion. The apparatus
is spent and cannot be rebuilt before delivery. Final policy dispatch has
an explicit last sender attack opportunity and subsequent causal closure.
This is finite project control, not dominion over the loser's entire future.
Merger and useful capture need their own actual authority/conversion law.

## 2. The staged continuation

A claim costs f and prepares an authenticated port. R can settle, attack
or request a stage at expense r_i. S can begin, refuse, wait or attack;
beginning and finishing each c_i consume separate positive installments.
R can interrupt halfway. A scheduled relay reveals completed progress at
the boundary, and final activation together with a fresh report. It does
not certify arbitrary programs or reveal the private state by itself.

Set D=(1-a)^2, g=(1-a)(2b-1-a), h=(1-a)(2b+1-a). For the peaceful core,

\[
\mu_i=1-\prod_{j=i}^N(1-r_j/D),\quad
q_i=1-e_{i-1}/g,\quad e_0=f,\ e_i=c_i,
\]

with the low claim/progress probabilities obtained by Bayes' rule in 073.
High S completes requested stages; low S sometimes mimics costly progress
and refuses final truthful activation. R sometimes settles early and
otherwise checks. C_a freely reports truth because that serves its new goal.
For a continuous policy interval, mu_1<=a makes policy 1 optimal at these
unchecked posteriors. Positive costs, remaining high adoption gains, initial
waiting incentives and the common belief sequence are separate conditions.

The effective verification expense is

\[
V_{eff}=D\left[1-\prod_i(1-r_i/D)\right].
\]

With constant effective combat damage, V_eff<d_R/(1-s) supplies the peaceful
region. Above that boundary the specified selected assessment uses early
combat screening. [075](../notes/075-stage-costs-combat-and-implementation-order.md)
derives the actual frequencies and the boundary mixtures; it does not
assert that every equilibrium fights. More stages can lower V_eff when
splitting adds no overhead. [090](../notes/090-ordering-work-under-a-changed-goal.md)
derives how feasible ordering affects founder welfare and literal completion.

[077](../notes/077-staged-assessment-and-controller-completion.md) supplies
the common completely mixed sequence and interruption checks. The exact
finite-tree audit covers every declared information set. Continuous
quadratic policies and low quote prices have separate analytical envelopes.
General programs and all bargaining windows are not enumerated by that tree.

## 3. Actual transfers and disagreement

A funded quote after refusal can trade current policy for remaining cash.
R's known-low quote has x=(b-a)-sqrt((b-a)^2-delta-e_S), policy a+x,
and price delta, on the budget-binding branch. Its net low loss is
ell=x^2-delta+e_R<0. The old sender's loss remains (b-a)^2 because R
extracts that trade surplus. Both transaction expenses are charged.

For original R with a=0, its unchecked policy is 1-mu, not 1. The smaller
root of

\[
\mu_i^2-(1-\ell+z_{i+1})\mu_i+r_i+z_{i+1}=0,
\quad z_i=\mu_i(1-\mu_i-\ell)/(1-\mu_i)
\]

determines the selected continuous disagreement continuation.
[080](../notes/080-continuous-policies-and-late-cash-bargaining.md) derives its
payoffs and full quote optimum. Money received earlier is at a remote
project; residual money opens the specified refusal window. More general
cash circulation and renewed offers require a new continuation.

## 4. A strong ordinary comparison, including destructive verification

The comparison retains original goals and grants arbitrary finite ordinary
communication, policy lotteries, type-independent burns and funded payments
t_0,t_1<=B. A binding policy/launch controller may use a contest to reveal
the state, and punish a captured false report by destroying up to P_max
additional sender output. This is stronger authority than the uncommitted
staged core. It receives free setup in the lower-bound comparison.

For original R target 0, set M=(1-s)(2b-1+P_max). If

\[
\eta\le\min\{p,p(d_S+\lambda d_R)/(d_S+M)\},
\qquad \lambda\ge1+\eta/(1-p),
\]

then every qualifying ordinary mechanism satisfies

\[
L_S+\lambda L_R\ge b^2+(2b-1)\eta
-\frac{b^2(1-p+\eta)^2}{(1-p)(1+\lambda)+\eta}
+(1-\lambda)B.
\tag{1}
\]

The proof couples low imitation of the high strategy, allowing the actual
state-sensitive capture penalty, and includes both parties' damage.
[093](../notes/093-remainder-certificate-for-the-ordinary-bound.md) gives the
complete nonnegative remainder. [085](../notes/085-sharp-choice-frontiers-and-goal-bargaining.md)
identifies attained audit and payment-slack segments. Useful confiscation,
an independent state sensor or protected truthful delegation changes the
comparison class. They are explicit alternatives, not silently ruled out.

## 5. An integrated, funded adoption family

Take b=1, B=.1899, f=.001, three c_i=1/150, three r_i=1/30000,
d_S>=5,d_R>=.01, R update expense .01 and early expense .001 per party.
R's original target is 0. Specialized sender stock 20 bounds additional
captured-state destruction by P_max<=15. State probabilities range over
[.45,.55], and 0<s<1 is arbitrary.

Pay T=B and choose the rational goal-bank value immediately below

\[
a(p)=\sqrt{-.1798+\frac{.1899-.011-.0001}{1-p}},
\]

on a grid of spacing 10^-5. Both original agents strictly prefer this
package to the actual continuous disagreement. It is peaceful, R genuinely
adopts before the state draw, and a high S genuinely adopts if challenged
through the last stage. Its sender loss is uniformly more than .00649
below every accepted ordinary control covered by (1), using the common
worst-case M=16. This is an interval proof with rational root enclosures,
not favorable sampling. [083](../notes/083-costed-combat-information-and-renegotiated-goals.md)
and 085 give the proof.

At p=.5,s=.05, the simple rational target a=.42 gives original losses
S=.869374 and R=-.090648, versus disagreement 1.010424 and -.089850.
The ordinary-control lower bound on accepted S loss is .880432. Actual
sender C activation has probability .478951; R's earlier update occurs on
every accepted branch. A receiver-proposer witness uses a=.22 and yields
S=1.005475,R=-.154650, strictly improving on its accepted ordinary frontier.

[084](../notes/084-prestate-completion-and-scope-of-the-offer-game.md) states
the conditional initial completion theorem: a finite public offer bank,
funded custody, two positive R installation installments, actual priced
recognition, bounds on remaining new authority costs, and selected consistent
continuations. These imply original-goal proposal/acceptance, non-atomic R
completion and the later current-self choices. The result is preserved by
any finite enlargement with the qualifying ordinary controls. The arbitrary
program wrapper is an analytical sufficient-condition argument, not a
hardware or unrestricted-program proof.

The first proposed package was displaced by a positive-cost punitive
ordinary controller. Renegotiating a and using the real budget repaired
that failure. [086](../notes/086-early-accommodation-and-a-repaired-disagreement.md)
then permits a cheap unilateral early quadratic accommodation family,
derives its improved disagreement, and repairs the joint package again.
The strict-truth variant has a uniform comparison gap exceeding .00244.
Cheap reusable and two-sided goal choice remains a larger game.

## 6. Combat values, protection and further accommodation

For current utility X_S+V(y), a known-low contest against offer y is
preferred exactly when sV(1)+(1-s)V(a)-V(y)>d_S. Increasing concave V
favors any sure offer above the contest mean. An achievement goal
V(y)=W*1{y>=z} instead makes subthreshold combat credible when sW>d_S.
[089](../notes/089-outcome-threshold-goals-and-combat.md) replaces the earlier
history-penalty threat by this outcome preference and verifies both regimes.

In the one-frame a=.3 example, a cheap credible threshold controller
induces selected preemptive combat with frequency .0457173. A causal exit
that protects valued outputs before impact restores peaceful C adoption;
R buys it when its real setup expense is below .000274538. The outcome-goal
successor declines ineffective launches because they cannot achieve its
project. Attacks remain available. A different physical recovery backup
in [078](../notes/078-late-threats-and-chosen-recovery.md) reduces extortion
and cost in two already-peaceful regimes; those benefits are kept distinct.

Further genuine accommodation can benefit a successor: in the known-low
quote family, changing target a to a/2 gives gain a^2/4-kappa when feasible.
Broader concave goals reach a^2/3-kappa. With a protected receiver fallback
of loss at most e and no new output, the joint-surplus theorem gives the
incumbent bound L_S>=a^2/2+kappa-e for *arbitrary* replacement criteria.
Thus kappa>a^2/2+e makes retention strict when current loss is a^2.
[091](../notes/091-joint-surplus-and-stable-outcome-goals.md) also constructs
a beneficial threshold successor with little remaining joint surplus and
a broad stability certificate. These are known-state subgame results;
they do not close the full private-information reusable-goal game.

Timely paid counteroffers can instead secure the same policy with the
incumbent goal and share the unspent installation expense. The exact
interval in [092](../notes/092-paid-counteroffers-before-goal-activation.md)
tests that repair before claiming actual completion. Fully informed,
cash-constrained ordinary trade also has a Pareto-efficient control in
091: costly goal changes cannot improve both originals there. Private
information and avoidable execution costs supply the additional joint
surplus in the constructive adoption family.

## 7. Verification, controls and unresolved scope

The scripts [staged assessment](../../experiments/r0004_staged_assessment.py)
and [funded certificates](../../experiments/r0004_funded_checks.py) use
fractions, one common perturbation, actual current evaluators and rational
root enclosures. They support self-checked proofs, not independent or
proof-assistant verification. The guard's launch-cost law and the finite
recovery calibration are separately labeled physical instances.

A matched protected sender delegate ties the final C update at equal cost.
Reproducing the entire package with fixed utilities also requires actual
receiver policy authority. Cheaper equivalent devices can displace the
corresponding update; equal-cost equivalence preserves voluntary genuine
adoption as an optimal choice. [088](../notes/088-assessment-audit-and-renegotiation-scope.md)
details these controls and distinguishes individual sequential choice from
renegotiation-proofness, coalition stability and population prevalence.

An open technical extension is one joint initial offer and continuation
with a priced reusable goal/safeguard menu and live funded counteroffers. Construct stable
current-goal accommodation and equally test obstructions after revising
targets, transfers, delegates and protection. Counterparty choice, renewal
and evolutionary prevalence remain subsequent explicit obligations.
The [2026-11-03 portfolio review](../reports/2026-11-03-scope-and-incentives-review.md)
prioritizes a broader productive/partner-choice environment next; it leaves
these E5 extensions open without making them prerequisites for that work.
