# E7 — exclusive final goals and overlapping production

**E7.0, r0006.** Start with the [readable findings](../reports/r0006-findings.md).
This is a family of research instances under C1, not a new mandatory
contract. The owner-directed focus is Blue/Red final goods with varying
shared manufacturing. Proofs are self-reviewed, not independently verified
or machine checked. The [run](../runs/r0006.md) records accounting and review.

## 1. Values, physical state and overlap dimensions

Original i maximizes E q_i, the actual final count of its own color,
wherever produced. It initially assigns zero terminal value to other colors.
Costs consume funded work/material or production time. Knowledge can enable
conversion of an existing finite reservoir into useful work; it does not
create material or conserved energy. Finishing capacity and available raw
inputs remain separate constraints. Multi-project versions specify actual
renewal, retained stocks and terminal conditions.

Useful process overlap has several coordinates:

| Coordinate | Meaning | Representative comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| theta | Fraction of setup work reused | Saved work theta F must exceed agreement work H, with downstream slack. |
| rho, h_ij | Usable additive knowledge | Reciprocal production gains; direction and asymmetry matter. |
| s=b/c, or CES r | Marginal productive response / substitutability | Changes how the partner responds to a public goal. |
| lambda, alpha, beta | Cross-use of captured or traded native assets | Changes both bargaining frontiers and the value of attack. |
| E, material, throughput | Finite productive scope and bottlenecks | Can exhaust or restrict the extra productive gain. |

A goal change is a changed ordering over feasible terminal lotteries,
including any declared history dependence. Positive affine recoding,
belief revision and a mere policy recommendation are different. Common
goals can be genuine even with separate controllers; this is not necessarily
a physical merger or a transfer of agency. Independent final idling, or
an explicit strict preference reversal, verifies genuineness in the examples.

## 2. A central exact productive class

For two private finishing lines use

`q_i=Q+a x_i+rho a x_j+b x_i x_j-c x_i^2/2-K_i`,

where a,c>0, rho in [0,1], s=b/c in (-1/2,1/2), t=a/c and A=a^2/c.
K_i is the actual paid implementation expense. Finite funded effort caps
are E=t(1+rho) for s<0, and at least t(1+rho)/(1-2s) for s>=0.
Define `ell=rho(1-s)+s`. Original effort is x_F=t/(1-s).

The common total-color goal has unique joint optimum

`x_C=t(1+rho)/(1-2s)`,
`Delta=A ell^2/[2(1-2s)(1-s)^2]` per original before costs.

Positive ell calls for more productive effort; negative ell calls for less
mutually costly interference. At ell=0 the original effort already maximizes
the total. In the additive case s=0, Delta=A rho^2/2.

An original choosing an independent public weight q_i+beta_i q_j,
beta_i in [0,1], has a unique zero-cost pure goal equilibrium
beta_i=s/(1-s) when s>0. It gives per-founder gain s(2-s)Delta.
For s<=0 and ell!=0, positive independent weights are displaced by retention.
A signed menu instead supports negative strategic weights when s<0, lowering
both founders' outputs; a reciprocal common agreement still supplies Delta.
Fixed update costs have exact retention/adoption coexistence thresholds.
These are different agreement spaces, with their own equilibria.
[Proofs](../notes/116-a-common-class-for-spillovers-and-complementarity.md),
[signed-menu check](../notes/122-signed-goal-menus-and-productive-competition.md).

## 3. From opportunity to actual reciprocal installation

Against an original counterpart in the stated production window, every
admissible unilateral goal or effort commitment is bounded by the original
best-response frontier. Its maximum gross gain is

`G_L=A s^2 ell^2/[2(1-2s^2)(1-s)^2]`.

For ell!=0,

`Delta-G_L=A ell^2(1+2s)/[2(1-2s)(1-2s^2)]>0`.

A paid effective-controller audit can activate a historical Ready event
only when both conditional common-goal templates and their actuator control
are valid. Preparations occur in stages without early observation; attack
and abort remain possible. The post-audit public production window and
failed-Ready continuation are specified. If each full expense is below
Delta-G_L and the actual outside alternatives are no better, reciprocal
installation is strictly preferred to unilateral Ready-failing production
deviations. Equal-cost equivalent ordinary devices can tie. The theorem is
about this audit interface, not arbitrary semantic verification or every
future renegotiation. [Statement and proof](../notes/131-reciprocal-adoption-against-broad-unilateral-choices.md).

Other links enlarge that implementation scope at explicit costs:

* Protected entry plus a chosen current-goal reservation bounds the first
  installer's original output against richer counteroffers (128). It proves
  a beneficial first change and a feasible two-change continuation; an ordinary
  counteroffer can change the selected number of genuine updates.
* A finite imperfect-audit game has strict mutual C when
  `K<A rho^2(1/2-epsilon)`. Paid inspection and mixed selection are solved
  separately (135). With post-detection repair rent d, the comparison becomes
  `A rho^2/2-K > epsilon A rho^2+(1-epsilon)d` (136).
* Asymmetric additive spillovers give a complete interior weighted productive
  frontier. Negotiated weights repair unequal benefits; actual convertible
  transfers can repair one-way sharing (137).

## 4. A broader productive continuation theorem

For compact product action sets and jointly concave nonnegative physical
counts, a strict funded Pareto opportunity gives a supported q*>r. With
positive supporting weights w and sufficiently small positive eta_i,

`G(q)=sum_i w_i q_i-sum_i eta_i(q_i-q_i*)^2`

is finite, increasing on the relevant output box and has q* as its unique
output maximum. It genuinely changes each original ordering when the
specified independent final idling is available. Differentiable concavity
of its action composition makes every common-goal effort equilibrium
produce q*. The realized-outcome utility is evaluated before expectation.
Initial adoption requires a separate interface. Nonsmooth essential-input
boundaries and newly available outside resources need their own checks.
[Class proof](../notes/134-a-concave-production-class-and-negotiated-goals.md).

The CES family supplies a second exact production response: for shared
index F_r, r<=1, the selected positive-continuation goal equilibrium is
`beta*=(1-r)/(3-r)`. At essential-input boundaries, observable sequential
effort repairs startup coordination and the ordinary move-order advantage
is recomputed. The exact minimum endpoint has ordinary efficient equilibria
already. These checks prevent a polynomial example from standing in for
every manufacturing technology (132).

## 5. Retained physical combat and chosen interventions

Battles remain stochastic, with strength-based winner probabilities, finite
material dispersal, useful-work loss, damaged infrastructure, recovery effort
and delay. The winner uses its current goal; defeat removes specified actuator
control, not the loser's valuations. Note 115 supplies a conservative finite
peace region. Its stronger full-site material bound covers attacks during
finishing as well. With protected completed stock, paced delivery preserves
remaining participation in the separate conversion model (129).

Native bundles r_B,r_R with symmetric cross-use lambda have peaceful
constraints q_B+lambda q_R<=r_B+lambda r_R and its counterpart. Original
war values are p delta(r_B+lambda r_R) and
(1-p)delta(r_R+lambda r_B). Exact frontier slacks price funded agreement.
Paid conversion investment, paid protection and earlier bargaining give
actual chosen-action conditions, including both combat and peaceful repairs.
No failure of a sufficient peace bound is itself labeled combat (117–121,129).

Conditional defensive goals can make destruction credible; an earlier
consensual common goal can save their expense. Contingent post-victory
concessions can evade weak defenses, and mixed offers can exploit an additive
attack bonus. Stronger weights and a statewise capped reservation provide
scoped successful repairs (133,139). Report these unilateral changes separately
from consensual accommodation and from original welfare.
