# E9 — what evidence would justify trusting an agreed change of goals?

Start with the [findings for readers](../reports/r0008-findings.md). The [research map](../MAP.md) places these results beside earlier work on conflict, productive cooperation and the fate of changed goals.

E9 is a family under the flexible [model contract](../MODEL.md). The [eighth recurrence](../runs/r0008.md) studies what a counterparty can know about the agreed ordering over outcomes, who deliberately tries to create that evidence, and what happens when an honest installation, a report or an actuator fails. The model keeps deliberate false claims, genuine attempts, successful installations, actual deliveries, disputes and both founders' original returns as separate outcomes.
