# E6 — renewable production, negotiated net gains and partner choice

**R0005 consolidated conditional model family.** Read the
[findings report](../reports/r0005-findings.md) for the explanation and
[run record](../runs/r0005.md) for accounting and the aim-preservation review.
The flexible contract C1 remains unchanged. E6 advances productive
continuation, current-goal consent, repeated choice and coalition boundaries;
it does not claim to solve unrestricted initial installation or market
formation. Review is contributing AI agent self-check, supported by exact
and finite-game checks. CG-03 remains blocked.

## 1. Shared definitions and the integrated question

Original welfare W_i is the agent's own delivered-project benefit minus
its real input and opportunity expenses. Most instances use linear value
in a common output unit; 104 studies nonlinear original values and the
corresponding certainty equivalents. Work, raw/organized/dispersed/spent
material, infrastructure and dated output remain distinct state variables.

An incumbent evaluates an installation using its current criterion. A
successor uses the installed criterion. Original welfare is reported
separately. Genuine change means a reversed ordering over outcome lotteries,
including deterministic outcomes; a new contract under one unchanged
accepted-contract rule is reuse, not another utility change.

The central conditional target is: **when a partner's future investment or
collaboration creates enough additional net value, negotiated goals can
protect the division of that value, make its production optimal, and pay
for their own installation under the originals' criteria.** Actual ordinary
controls, funding, recognition and initial staging determine whether that
opportunity is selected. Notes 094–106 develop and repair a service-market
route; 107–111 connect it to complementary groups, investment and renewal.

## 2. A common-maximum certificate for genuine negotiated goals

Let positive weights gamma_i sum to one. For actual net gains W, install

    G_i=(1-beta_i)W_i
           +beta_i gamma_i min_j(W_j/gamma_j),
    1-gamma_i<beta_i<1.                              (E6.1)

The criteria are monotone and concave. For every realized outcome,

    G_i(W)<=gamma_i sum_j W_j.                       (E6.2)

If the actual attainable net total is at most T* and an executable plan
delivers W_i=gamma_i T*, it attains every G_i's global maximum. No later
goal can improve its predecessor through another outcome law in that
physical class. With strict coefficients, indifference preserves balanced
gains and maximal expected total. At fixed total, the original prefers a
larger own share while G_i prefers the negotiated balance: this is genuine
preference change. [107](../notes/107-reusable-goals-across-several-partners.md)
and [108](../notes/108-productive-capital-and-dynamic-balanced-goals.md) prove
the bound, its weighted version, lotteries and the finite common-maximum
continuation argument. Equality beta_i=1-gamma_i permits additional ties.

This is an implementation certificate once the balanced plan is physically
attainable. It supplies neither an initial launch technology nor a unique
initial agreement. Dated-flow and lifetime-gain versions are different;
efficient dated risk sharing must actually be available for 108's dynamic
version. Equal expected receipts alone are insufficient for that certificate.

## 3. Productive investment and the original adoption budget

In [110](../notes/110-investment-surplus-and-voluntary-productive-partners.md),
P prepares an owner's project with effort e, cost I(e)=ce^2/2 and output ae.
Write A=a^2/c and T(e)=ae-I(e). Both can attack the prepared workshop;
P's victory probability is p, the recoverable prize is chi ae, and each
spends finite consumables. Battle damages the preparation line beyond
the current deadline; raw and spent stocks remain accounted for. The
different capital kernel in 108 permits rebuilding after combat.

Put theta=p chi. In the specified ordinary owner-proposer continuation,
P receives max(0,theta ae-d_P), with acceptance at reservation explicitly
selected. When d_P<theta^2 A/2,

    e_F=theta a/c,
    U_O^F=theta(1-theta)A+d_P,
    U_P^F=theta^2 A/2-d_P.                           (E6.3)

Ordinary play is peaceful but underinvests. Negotiate gamma_O=tau,
gamma_P=1-tau in (E6.1). Efficient effort e*=a/c and transfer
I(e*)+(1-tau)T* attain both current-goal maxima, where T*=A/2.
The originals can both strictly gain after actual setup K_O,K_P when

    2theta(1-theta)+2(d_P+K_O)/A < tau
       < 1-theta^2+2(d_P-K_P)/A.                    (E6.4)

The interval has positive width exactly when

    K_O+K_P < A(1-theta)^2/2.                        (E6.5)

It must also beat actual outside partners and ordinary controllers. The
right side is lost productive surplus, not a reward for becoming corrigible.
The continuation proof covers partial effort, transfers, disposal, combat
and arbitrary subsequent goal outcomes within the stated frontier. A finite
implementation containing the optimum admits the productive continuation.
Initial reliable staged activation before investment remains a separate
interface. Its costs cannot be set to zero merely because the goals exist.

For a=2,c=1,p=1/4,chi=4/5,d_i=1/50, ordinary effort is 2/5 and original
returns are (33/50,3/50). Equal negotiated net gains induce effort 2 and
return (1,1) before setup, or (9/10,9/10) after K_i=1/10. The investor
must fund preparation cost 2 before reimbursement. Both selected paths
are peaceful; the measured change is greater productive cooperation and
genuine preference change.

The construction extends to concave output and convex preparation cost.
With several investors, funded proportional net gains make each maximize
the same total-surplus function. Smooth strict concavity gives a unique
effort equilibrium in that balanced-division continuation. Exact zero-input
complementarity can retain a coordination equilibrium. These statements
are separate from unique adoption or market selection.

## 4. Revisions, liquidity and private information

An owner already valuing equal gross receipts can approve a further genuine
net-gain goal because it induces additional investment. In 110 the new
share interval is 1/2+2k_O/A<tau<3/4-2k_P/A, nonempty for k_O+k_P<A/8.
This uses the incumbent's gross-receipt criterion. Giving the investor a
gross-receipt fairness criterion can instead make the lower investment its
current maximum; balancing gross receipts and balancing net gains differ.

With preparation budget B, the changed-goal effort is min(a/c,sqrt(2B/c)).
If profitable ordinary investment already exhausts the same budget, there
is no extra investment surplus to fund positive setup. A real owner-funded
advance L with opportunity expense rL has the interior optimum

    B+L*=A/[2(1+r)^2].                              (E6.6)

Both installed participants prefer a funded advance when it raises their
shared net value. Ordinary loans, collateral and delegation must also be
compared. At fixed B, efficiency can first expand and then contract the
incremental adoption budget as both methods hit the common constraint.

Known genuinely installed net-gain goals support truthful reporting of a
private investment cost through the common-maximum bound. An original
choosing an unverified imputed cost can prefer distortion. Certification of
the program label is not certification of its true cost reference. The
full goal-selection/screening game remains open; 110 gives exact comparisons.

## 5. Renewable opportunities and actual partner choice

If a reusable goal supplies actual per-cycle advantage Delta over the best
available ordinary route, n renewed projects justify cost K when
A_n Delta>K. Fresh input flows or investment laws must supply those projects.
Common-state Markov opportunities instead give the stopping equation

    D=min(k,Delta+delta P D),                        (E6.7)

with adoption at k<=Delta+delta P D. More available partners favor adoption
when they improve the goal-enabled menu more than the ordinary menu.
[099](../notes/099-renewal-adoption-and-voluntary-assignment.md) and
[103](../notes/103-renewable-markets-and-the-option-to-wait.md) derive the
stationary, search and observed-arrival cases, including chosen paid access.

When the routes change future transitions, use

    U=r_G+delta P_G U,
    D=min(k,H+delta P_F D),
    H=r_G-r_F+delta(P_G-P_F)U.                       (E6.8)

[111](../notes/111-cooperation-that-changes-future-opportunities.md) derives
the exact qualification threshold and shows how faster ordinary access can
displace an update while improving welfare. These are implemented-route
comparisons, not assumed population distributions or an automatic effect
of intelligence.

For a willing productive partner j, funded share negotiation can improve
both originals when T_j*-K_Oj-K_j>v_O+b_j, where v_O and b_j are their
actual alternatives. Exclusive assignment and noncrowding complementary
networks have different stability certificates (099/107). Their core
allocations do not by themselves solve installation, bargaining or attacks
on outsiders.

## 6. Combat, coalitions and useful repairs

The workshop kernel in
[098](../notes/098-renewable-resources-combat-and-fundable-production.md)
computes recoverable output Y from finite material, work and time. Ordinary
division exists when h<=R-Y, before extra funding constraints. At high
settlement cost, partial productive service can bridge the shortfall when
(1-c/b)(d_j-a_j)>h-(R-Y)+K. Notes 100–105 test retaliation, funded bonds,
later goals, fee exposure, slow/fast disposal and a finite revocable-trade
bound. Those repairs preserve ordinary successes and narrow the claimed
utility-change advantage.

In the renewable-capital example, patience raises both productive and
post-combat rebuilding values. Its effect on a fixed offered change has
sign gamma_i-p_i. Renegotiated shares can preserve consent even when a fixed
equal division loses it. Faster reconstruction can reduce the avoided-loss
advantage while raising both productive values (108).

[109](../notes/109-partner-coalitions-and-the-scope-of-concern.md) compares
productive surplus M with coalition capture values. Additive strength
supports strength-proportional peaceful shares. For symmetric n-agent
contests, the complete declared coalition condition is

    M >= max_{1<=k<n} (n/k)(p_k L-c_k).              (E6.9)

Concern confined to a profitable attacking coalition can favor combat.
Broader concern can restore peace, but a winning defender's voluntary
compensation can also insure an attacker. The three-agent example has
explicit peace/combat/restraint regions as concern varies. A guarded
compensation rule repairs direct attack; a cheap further threat can extract
tribute under that guard. The stronger common-maximum criterion covers
those tribute outcomes, while its initial adoption still needs consent.

## 7. Evidence, control comparison and open integrated link

Claims R501–R522 and assumptions A102–A120 record the statements and limits.
The five `test_e6_*.py` files check analytical boundaries, independent
finite-game responses, physical accounts and preserved counter-deviations.
These are self-checks, not independent or machine-assisted formal proofs.
The literature map distinguishes conventional fairness, investment,
assignment, contest and stopping machinery from this project's application.

The achieved link is **productive opportunity → specified genuine goals →
optimal continued production and current-goal revision bounds → conditional
original consent and renewal/partner comparisons**. Actual initial launch,
beliefs about qualified goals, competing coalition formation and unrestricted
physical outside options remain open interfaces. A matching ordinary
controller can tie or displace a goal package at its actual cost. No merger,
population-selection or synthesis claim follows from this recurrence.
