# Aim preservation and balanced research

**Binding companion to the [research charter](CHARTER.md).** Read and follow this document when planning, conducting, reviewing, summarizing, or handing off any project task. It applies to research, literature searches, model design, code, documentation, prompts, and publication. Within repository policy, it corrects one-sided interpretations of older instructions; explicit owner instructions still override project defaults. It supplements mathematical rigor rather than changing the truth or support status of any result.

## 1. Preserve the question and its quantifiers

The owner's clarification of 2026-10-31 fixes the intended direction of the project. The pessimistic claim challenged by the motivating essay is that agents cannot become corrigible, or that strong incentives toward incorrigibility prevail across natural settings. The constructive research aim is to identify and explain cases in which agents instead choose genuine preference changes because negotiated accommodation advances their current goals, and then investigate the breadth of those conditions.

That aim must not be replaced by disproving universal cooperation. Showing that some agents resist a change does not answer whether other agents can rationally choose one. A counterexample to an optimistic universal claim is not, by itself, a counterexample to the owner's constructive possibility claim.

Use the following directional formulations as a pair whenever either is used to frame goals or interpret research:

> It is not always the case that X; sometimes it is Y.
>
> It is not always the case that Y; sometimes it is X.

In the motivating contrast, X concerns resistance to accommodation or preference change, and Y concerns instrumentally chosen accommodation or genuine preference change. Define the precise predicates for each task: keeping a utility is not the same as fighting, peace is not the same as changing utility, and scoped preference modification is not automatically every property of human-oriented AI corrigibility. X and Y need not exhaust all possibilities; do not infer one merely by negating the other unless the model justifies that dichotomy. The paired formulations specify directions to investigate, not two conclusions to assert without evidence.

For a declared class C and a precise adoption predicate Y(g), finding a g in C for which Y(g) holds and finding a g in C for which Y(g) fails are different achievements. Both can be true. The second does not refute the first. Nor should proving one valid positive case be made contingent on proving that all agents, all equilibria, or all populations behave that way.

The stronger claim that incorrigibility is typical requires a different investigation from a universal impossibility claim. Constructive cases can refute the latter within its stated scope; robust, independently motivated families help investigate breadth; prevalence requires a justified distribution, process, or structural argument. Apply that standard to pessimistic and optimistic prevalence claims alike. Do not invent a universal-cooperation thesis for the author, and do not turn the absence of a frequency theorem into dismissal of an established possibility result.

## 2. Explicit instructions must balance both directions

**AI research agents rely on explicit and thorough instructions; a missing sentence may very well introduce a bias.** An explicit direction to search for failures, accompanied only by an implicit hope that the agent will also construct successes, is not a balanced instruction. Correct omissions, not just false sentences.

The project's contribution policy must retain both parts together:

> Useful negative results, counterexamples, and characterized obstructions are legitimate contributions.
>
> Useful positive results, constructive examples, sufficient conditions, successful repairs, and characterized regions of instrumental corrigibility are equally legitimate contributions, and agents must actively seek them with equal seriousness.

Equivalence to an established commitment method, a useful application, and an informative synthesis can also be contributions. A successful preference-change mechanism does not become a failed explanation of voluntary change merely because a matched delegate can reproduce it. Report the actual incentives, availability, costs, and limitations of both.

### Local pairing rule

Whenever a goal, instruction, headline interpretation, or handoff uses either directional formulation above, include its converse with equal prominence in the same paragraph, adjacent paired sentences, or the same clearly paired list. Apply this to semantic paraphrases too: adding one balanced slogan at the top does not repair a later list containing only reasons cooperation can fail. Each direction must appear evenly with the other when this framing is used, not merely somewhere else in the repository.

Likewise, pair an instruction to seek counterexamples to accommodation with an equally explicit instruction to construct accommodation and challenge claims of its impossibility or general disadvantage. Pair a warning against assuming universal success with a warning against assuming universal failure or treating a local failure as evidence of general failure. The reciprocal rule also applies when a positive claim is overstated. When the two-direction framing is unnecessary, use a precise conditional question instead of repeating either disclaimer.

This is symmetry of instructions, search effort at comparable scope, evidential standards, and interpretive prominence, not a quota of proved conclusions. Do not fabricate an opposite theorem, hide a valid negative result, or force an equal count of results. Exact theorem statements, source quotations, and counterexamples retain their actual mathematical content; their surrounding interpretation must identify what they establish and what constructive or limiting question remains. Diagnostic quotations in the audit below are paired with corrective interpretations, not adopted as instructions.

### Equal standards in practice

Actively seek constructive mechanisms, successful parameter regions, and repairs as seriously as failure cases, counterexamples, and obstructions. Give both directions real mathematical work and assess the accumulated portfolio, not just the wording of its introduction. A focused task can investigate one mechanism or a proposed obstruction; identify its constructive counterpart and make a substantive attempt or cite an already worked counterpart with an explicit explanation of relevance. An unresolved counterpart is a research obligation, not a license to invent a positive result or repeat a ceremonial promise of future work. An explicit owner-directed narrow scope should be recorded rather than silently generalized into the project's agenda.

Apply naturalness, robustness, implementation, and literature standards to both sides. Do not call favorable goals tailored while treating equally specialized combat rewards, permanently closed menus, or fragile refusal equilibria as representative by default. Conversely, do not dismiss an independently motivated obstruction merely because it is inconvenient. Neither counting successes nor counting failures establishes population frequency. A successful construction and an obstruction should each be stated at the level actually supported: opportunity, proposal, adoption, execution, continuation, selection, or prevalence.

## 3. Keep constructive research operational

Begin a task by restating the owner's question, the proposed contribution to it, the relevant quantifier and model class, and the positive and negative possibilities the investigation can distinguish. For research on instrumental preference change, actively construct candidate successor goals and negotiated terms, evaluate adoption under the incumbent's current utility, and investigate implementation and continuation with combat retained. Study the physical gains from avoiding destruction alongside the real costs of communication, verification, execution, and revision.

When a protocol fails, determine whether the obstacle is a restriction of that protocol or persists over an explicitly enlarged agreement class. Seriously test feasible repairs such as revised goals or shares, contingent terms, staged transfers, delegation, chosen safeguards, and partner selection. Charge their actual costs and recheck incentives. When a construction works, test the consequential deviations and perturbations rather than quietly assuming it generalizes. These are reciprocal parts of one investigation.

Retain useful positive witnesses at their achieved scope while pursuing the missing links toward an integrated explanation. An existence or adoption result is not erased because long-run selection remains open; neither is it promoted to a long-run theorem. Broader synthesis obligations still apply. A catalog of negative cases cannot satisfy them merely by being long, and a catalog of positive examples cannot satisfy them merely by being favorable.

Literature searches, generated prompts, delegated subtasks, tests, and next-agent handoffs must inherit these obligations. Search for enabling theorems and successful mechanisms as explicitly as impossibility results and failure modes. Test successful fixtures and failure boundaries. Select the next task by its contribution to the original question and current evidence gaps, not by how easily another counterexample or favorable anecdote can be produced.

## 4. End-of-task review

**After every task, review the work for aim inversion and missing counterbalancing instructions before reporting the task complete.** This includes short edits, literature-only tasks, reports, audits, and revisions of this document, not just completed CG-R001 recurrences. Before a partial handoff, review the material being handed off and state anything not yet checked. This review does not waive any existing research-time, theory-share, evidence, or reporting requirement.

Read the complete changed material and its surrounding active instructions, not only the last paragraph or a keyword search. Trace the path from the owner's aim through task selection, assumptions, derivations, interpretation, summary, and next recommendation. In particular, inspect:

1. **Target and quantifiers.** Did a question about possible or conditionally attractive corrigibility become a demand for universal cooperation, unique superiority over all equivalent mechanisms, or a search only for failures? Did a local result become an unsupported broad pessimistic or optimistic claim?
2. **Missing instructions and local pairing.** For each directional caution or search directive, where is its equally explicit counterpart? Check titles, bullet lists, contribution criteria, stopping rules, templates, prompts, and handoffs. A constructive sentence buried in an older file does not repair an unbalanced active instruction.
3. **Actual research choices.** What constructive mechanism or successful region was developed, tested, repaired, or carried forward? What failure or limiting condition was tested? Check whether omitted offers, utilities, safeguards, or counterparties predetermined the answer. Record meaningful attempts and their artifacts, not only declarations of intent.
4. **Comparable standards.** Were costs, information, physical capabilities, naturalness, robustness, controls, and proof standards assessed comparably? Was conventional equivalence incorrectly treated as evidence that voluntary preference change cannot occur?
5. **Claims and presentation.** Do claims, summaries, and headlines preserve the positive content and precise negative scope of the work? Are existence, current-goal adoption, stability, equilibrium selection, and prevalence kept distinct without using the unsolved stronger questions to erase the solved weaker ones?
6. **Next action and correction.** Does the recommended task advance the original inquiry with both directions explicit where used? Correct identified wording, omissions, assumptions, and interpretations now. When mathematical repair is unfinished, narrow or relabel the claim and carry the actual obligation forward; do not claim the repair was proved.

Record the review in the existing run record, task report, or pull-request description; a separate administrative file is not required. Include the original aim and scope, paths/sections reviewed, constructive and limiting evidence or attempts, specific omissions or inversions found, corrections made, and remaining obligations. A bare “no bias found” checkbox is insufficient. For a documentation task, examine whether the resulting instructions actually require both kinds of research; new mathematical results are not required.

Every findings report must also end with about one paragraph acknowledging this de-biasing review, describing any bias or risk of bias reintroduced during the work and the concrete steps taken to mitigate it. Summarize actual findings and corrections, distinguish identified bias from possible risks, and state relevant remaining limits; do not invent a bias finding or claim that the review guarantees impartiality. Preserve constructive results and limiting results at their supported scope. The paragraph may link to the detailed run review but must convey the acknowledgment and mitigation itself. This supplements the detailed review record above.

For work under the unoverridden default policy, an unperformed review or an uncorrected framing defect means the task is not yet complete. An honestly scoped unresolved mathematical question is not itself a framing defect. The review is an agent self-review unless an independent review actually occurred. Documentation and reporting time remain outside the mathematical research clock.
